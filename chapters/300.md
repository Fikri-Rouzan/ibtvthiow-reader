"Mmmmm..."

On another clear day at the Hero Association office.

Stardus sat there with her lips tightly sealed, seriously contemplating something.

Specifically, how to approach Egostic...

'....'

...Actually, when she thought about it, was there even anything left to approach? What kind of relationship did they have? They had saved each other's lives and shared countless experiences together.

The problem was that it was a relationship between a hero and a villain.

"Sigh..."

...She just wanted to spend more time with Egostic. That's all. Why was that so difficult?

Because he was a villain and she was a hero? Or was it because Egostic wasn't very active, so there were few opportunities to see him?

...As she continued these thoughts—which no one would believe were a hero's thoughts about a villain—she eventually clenched her fist and made a resolution.

'Right. Next time we meet...!'

She would take the initiative.

Though she hadn't thought at all about what exactly she would take initiative on or how she would do it... that was her resolution nonetheless.

Hadn't Lee Seol-ah advised her to do the same?

But in reality, this conversation could only happen when Egostic caused a terror attack.

Since he had tricked her and escaped in Busan last time, he had vanished without a trace.

Right. He had taken that girl Ariel with him.

...Thinking about that again made Stardus feel inexplicably boiling inside, but she decided to endure it. In truth, there wasn't much she could do besides endure it unless she put everyone in prison... though that wouldn't be easy either since it would likely make Egostic sad.

"Egostic... when will you come?"

And so, during these unexpectedly peaceful days with no terror attacks for three consecutive days.

Today too, a hero was waiting for a villain's terror attack.

As she leaned back in her chair, her blonde hair flowing down, quietly lost in thought.

Suddenly, something occurred to her.

'That's right. Come to think of it...'

Wasn't this exactly when Egostic would usually launch a terror attack?

When she had waited and waited, and finally started to think he wasn't coming... that's when Egostic would always appear on TV.

This was an intuition she had as an Egostic expert.

And.

Several hours later that day.

"Seriously, she only smiles brightly without even realizing it when Egostic launches a terror attack..."

...Someone who always turns serious and scary when other villains cause terror attacks.

The association staff member thought this as they left the room.

...Incidentally, almost all the staff at the association seriously believed the rumors of a romance between Egostic and Stardus.

The terror attack by Egostic that continued for several hours proceeded quite conventionally.

Bombs falling with the bank as a backdrop, Stardus blocking them, Egostic taunting her to catch him...

He was handling teleportation quite skillfully, and as Stardus blocked all the bombs, quite a bit of time passed.

Of course, Egostic was throwing bombs one by one in a way that made them easy to catch, so the terror attack itself was easy to stop. According to a message from an association staff member, the money seemed to be fake, so she could focus on stopping the attack without worrying about burning it.

As time passed, Egostic also seemed quite tired.

But seeing him like that made Stardus even more anxious.

'...How am I supposed to approach him here?'

The situation was that he would run away if she approached.

To make matters worse, today's terror attack seemed to be coming to an end. There were no casualties and minimal damage, but... for some reason, she felt sad.

"Haa, haa. I'm getting tired now..."

"Then, just surrender already...!"

As she said this, Stardus reached out toward him as he wobbled in the air, his speed slowing down.

She already knew. That he would escape using teleportation like this.

Still, she had to try until the end—that was her mindset.

However.

Grab.

"Huh?"

Her hand, which she thought would slice through empty air, somehow caught his arm.

"Oh my, I've been caught. Haha."

Seeing him standing there, caught by the arm, almost in an embrace, smiling awkwardly as if troubled while sweating from exhaustion.

"...Huh?"

Stardus, making eye contact with him, unconsciously let out a dumbfounded sound.

"I surrender. It's my first defeat."

"...Uh, you're not running away?"
