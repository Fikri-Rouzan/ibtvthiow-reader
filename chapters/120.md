Chae Na-young, who had been clicking her mouse while looking at her computer, suddenly shouted without realizing it.

Then, with tears in her eyes, she blocked the comment section of the video.

The problematic video that forced her to take the drastic measure of blocking comments was a criticism of Egostic.

"Haah... Really, why...."

She sighed deeply.

Chae Na-young is a journalist.

With barely enough subscribers to make ends meet, it was currently her only hope.

She was working hard to maintain the channel while also working part-time at a convenience store. And now, her newly uploaded video was being bombarded with dislikes.

"...No, is Egostic some kind of sacred cow? A sacred cow, I tell you!"

The video she had uploaded this time was criticizing the villain Egostic.

To be precise, it was criticizing the people who mindlessly followed him. I mean, he's a villain, right? So why don't mainstream broadcasters criticize him more harshly?

So she decided to take the initiative and pioneer this blue ocean.

And the result was...

A complete disaster.

"....Ha. I've failed. This was my only hope..."

She thought to herself and unconsciously began to sob.

Her view counts were already declining with each video. Now she was truly at risk of not being able to make a living.

On the verge of not being able to pay her rent, she was about to collapse from the dislikes and malicious comments.

"...Ah. Should I just quit everything?"

Haha, it probably won't work out.

Maybe I should have bought a lottery ticket.
