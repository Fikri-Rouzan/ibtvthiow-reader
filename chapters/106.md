Chapter 106 - Dark Night

\# Pink Storm Aftermath

Several hours had passed since the pink storm devastated downtown Seoul.

Moonlight

The last thing anyone saw was two glowing pink characters shooting across the dark evening sky.

The villain who caused all this chaos had vanished without a trace, along with the pink energy she had summoned.

And only heroes remained where the villain had departed.

"How could I... ME of all people... be so useless at night... This is impossible!! Aaaaargh... I should just go die. Why am I even alive?"

Shadow Walker was crouched in a corner, digging at the ground in despair.

"Why isn't Da-in answering her phone? Sigh... come on..."

And in another corner, Icicle was muttering with his phone pressed against his face, anxiously trying to call someone.

And in the middle of it all.

Stardust stood there with the most dejected expression, staring at the spot where the villain had disappeared.

"......"

The place where the villain had been was now in ruins.

This city, which had been bustling with people just earlier today, was destroyed in a single night.

In this desolate place with no one around, only debris scattered about.

Shin Ha-ru clenched her fist and gritted her teeth.

....The destruction of the city wasn't the problem. Buildings could be rebuilt.

The problem was that despite all their efforts, they couldn't capture the villain.

And that villain, who had vanished after shooting only the word "Moonlight" into the sky, would likely return.

And the biggest problem was:

When that villain returned to cause more terror, she would probably still be unable to stop it.

"....."

The moment she realized this fact again.

An indescribable feeling of despair washed over her.

That villain would probably return.

And again harm people and destroy their homes.

And she would still be just as powerless.

Lost in these thoughts, she suddenly looked up at the sky.

She had felt this kind of frustration before.

But back then, someone had been there to encourage her.

And sometimes, that person had stepped in directly to prevent disasters.

Though she hadn't fully realized it at the time.

That person had been a greater support than she'd thought.

Without him, how terribly things could unfold.

Only in his absence did she now understand.

"....."

And.

Realizing he wouldn't come this time.

Realizing he probably wouldn't come next time either.

That fact, without her even knowing why, made her sad.

Though it wasn't something to be sad about, she couldn't help it.

Under the dark night sky, facing the ruins spread before her.

She just stared.

Just kept staring blankly.
