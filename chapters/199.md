The real battle had finally begun.

\***

In truth, Shin Ha-ru had been somewhat nervous about seeing Egostic again.

That moment in front of the so-called demon castle, when he came to save her.

She couldn't help but smile, her heart beating slightly faster. His back view was so reassuring.

Even though she understood intellectually that he was a villain and tried to be hostile, her heart didn't dislike him.

That's why Shin Ha-ru was nervous about seeing Egostic again. She wasn't sure how she would react.

What should she say when she sees him? No, how would he approach her? ...Probably, if he acted like the usual Egostic, he would behave exactly the same as before...

She had actually spent time deeply pondering why Egostic carried out terrorist attacks, his relationships with the women around him, and reasons why he might not be a villain... but ultimately, all of that was part of figuring out what face she should show him when they met again.

Anyway, that day finally came. The day Egostic finally launched a new terror attack.

And.

Stardus forgot all the worries she'd been having and just stared in bewilderment at the scene before her eyes.

...Where on earth did he get something like that dragon?

In the sky where heavy snow and ice particles mixed and fell.

When she flew there and was stunned to see him riding a dragon.

Egostic, seeing her, began to speak.

Words about this dragon being Ego-stream's new member, about conquering Seoul... words that would have angered and antagonized her if spoken by any other villain, but somehow felt familiar coming from his mouth.

...Regardless, Stardus still stood there slightly dazed. ...Dragon aside, now that she was face to face with Egostic again, seeing his smiling face and eyes, she didn't know what to do. Should she be angry about the terrorist attack? Or should she ask about last time? She had many things she wanted to say, but her thoughts were tangled.

And what snapped her out of this confusion was Egostic's next words.

"Ms. Stardus. Even someone like you won't be able to withstand my attack. You seem to have been quite active lately, but I cannot acknowledge that."

"....Hmm?"

"Well, I might acknowledge you if you manage to stop me here... but that's impossible. How could a human possibly stop a dragon!"

He laughed "Haha-!" as he said that.

And at that moment, she realized Egostic's intention.

Ah, he's telling me to stop him. He wants me to try and stop this dragon.

You can do it, Stardus.

And then, she unconsciously recalled an old memory.

Yes. Egostic had always been like this. From the beginning until now, he always threw some kind of trial at her and told her to overcome it.

...It felt familiar somehow.

And thinking about that, Stardus—Shin Ha-ru—realized:

I don't even think of Egostic's terrorist attacks as terrorism anymore.

I think of what he causes as trials given to me.

...I don't know what the truth is. Maybe it really is terrorism.

But does that even matter?

In the end, this too is something that he, Egostic, prepared just for me.

Yes.

If he prepared this so diligently.

I should also do my best to face it.

And I should show him that I can overcome it.

Since that's what he wants.

Thinking this, Stardus unconsciously smiled and said:

"Alright, I understand. I just need to stop you here, right? Like last time."

And seeing Egostic grin at her response, she was convinced.

Indeed, this is what he wants.

Well, then I have no choice but to act accordingly.

With that mindset, she clenched her fist, emitting light. To stop his "terrorism," and to win against him.

She took flight.
