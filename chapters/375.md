\# Breaking News: S-Rank Hero Stardust Defeats Europe's Most Feared Villain

Castrum, Europe's worst villain who even five S-rank heroes together could barely force to retreat, has finally fallen.

Known as the Master of the Fortress, he had terrorized European society with his incredible endurance that never faltered and overwhelming attacks.

And now, he has finally been defeated.

Not after a fierce battle... but by a single hero. In just minutes, in an instant.

And that hero?

She was an S-rank hero from a previously quiet country in the East.

A member of the Korean Hero Association.

S-rank hero, Stardust.

The woman who defeated Castrum with just a few punches.

In an instant, she became the talk of the world, including Europe.

[Breaking news! Castrum, known as Europe's worst villain who terrorized European society, has fallen. The fact that he was defeated by a Korean hero named Stardust is causing a huge shock. What is the identity of this woman who defeated in just minutes someone that multiple S-rank heroes couldn't beat together? Today's news is about Stardust...]

Immediately becoming the top story on every news outlet's prime time broadcast.

The entire world turned its attention to this superhuman hero who appeared like a comet.

[Actually, this isn't the first time Stardust has made headlines. She previously stopped an attack by the religious group Wolganggyo. She was already a ranked hero, but since she only operated in her home country, people weren't aware of how strong she really was...]

[Experts analyzing the footage say Stardust possesses 'strength that defies common sense' and appears to be superior in one-on-one combat compared to any hero we've seen so far...]

Honestly, it was kind of funny how everyone was getting so fired up over defeating just one villain. No matter how strong he was, it was still just one villain, and this alone wasn't enough for a complete evaluation.

However.

A villain so powerful that European heroes ranked in the top 10 couldn't defeat him even after gathering together and fighting for hours, only managing to exhaust him enough to make him retreat.

'Hnnnngh!'

'Aaaaarrrrggghhh!'

The sight of him being pathetically sent flying by just a few punches from her and collapsing was enough to stir the hearts of many people.

And this...

...naturally caused an even bigger commotion in her home country, which had known about Stardust all along.

Especially the nationalist YouTube channels.

"Wow."

In the Ego-stream living room.

After checking the news on YouTube, I squinted at the recommended videos.

...The titles alone gave me a headache. How much fabricated content is in those titles? Besides, I've never really watched that kind of content, so why is it showing up in my recommendations?

Putting down my phone, I crossed my legs, sighed, and fell into thought.

...Actually, not everything in those nationalist videos is completely wrong.

After all, Stardust is currently the center of attention. The whole world must be watching her, right?

The current talk among people goes like this:

'Stardust might be the strongest among all heroes.'

Heroes all have different abilities. There are heroes specialized in strength like Stardust, but there are also heroes with special abilities. Like Dungeon Maker among ability users, who can create and trap people in alternate dimensions.

Because of this, it's impossible to rank heroes simply by strength. Ex Machina, who has no physical power but can turn back time, was unofficially considered the number one hero until now.

However.

The strength Stardust displayed was absolutely overwhelming.

Against Castrum, who specialized in defense and was famous for his endurance, even winning after hours of neck-and-neck fighting would be considered impressive.

But defeating him in just a few minutes with one or two punches?

'She's on a different level.'

Everyone couldn't help but think that.

That's why the world was showing such intense interest.

Just how strong is this hero called Stardust?

In the hero ranking where the top position constantly changes without any hero particularly standing out, no one has shown such overwhelming skill as her until now.

"Ughhhh."

While I was thinking about this.

...Seo-eun, who was checking public opinion on her smartphone from under the sofa, handed me her phone with a groan.

"...Haah. People are really going crazy. Especially your fan cafe and everyone's obsession... ugh."

She said, handing me her phone.
