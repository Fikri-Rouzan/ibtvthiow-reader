"Sigh..."

Stardust turned her gaze away from Egostic's fan cafe with a sigh.

She had checked it out of habit, hoping for some information, but most posts were just lamenting his absence from terror activities.

"..."

Unconsciously stroking her cup, she took a sip of cold water and gathered her thoughts.

Egostic not showing up... well, that was nothing new. She wasn't particularly anxious about it. Though her eyes kept drifting to the calendar, that probably had nothing to do with it. Probably.

"...Haah."

Sighing without realizing it, Stardust fell into thought again.

Egostic is a villain. He must be a villain since he's registered as one by the Association.

But.

"..."

Why does she think about Egostic more than other heroes do?

Why does her heart ache when he gets hurt, and why does she feel strange when he looks only at her?

Why can't she bring herself to hate him?

...I don't know.

Let's think about that later.

...I don't even know what face to make when I see Egostic during his next terror act. I guess I should just confront him as usual. That seems to be what he wants.

"...I should get back to work."

With another sigh, she turned back to her monitor and resumed working.

She was reviewing footage of a previous battle with a villain.

It was someone called Crankwroci who wore a green bodysuit and shot red beams from his eyes, and he had been quite difficult to deal with.

...Even though Stardust herself had become much stronger than before... somehow the villains were also getting stronger, so the effort required remained about the same.

'...What if I hadn't gotten stronger at all and remained as weak as before?'

Stardust shuddered at the thought. ...It was something she didn't even want to imagine. If that had been the case, how terrible it would have been. With so many villains appearing each week, the Republic of Korea would have been destroyed long ago.

As she was lost in these terrible thoughts, Stardust played the video of her battle with the green villain again to review the fight.

...It wasn't interesting. Watching her fights with Egostic never felt this way, even after multiple viewings. Strange feeling. Perhaps real combat is best after all.

As she was continuing her thoughts while watching the boring footage...

Suddenly, her office door burst open, and an Association employee rushed in, panting.

"S-Stardust!"

"Yes?"

As she responded, Stardust felt her heart racing without realizing it.

When an employee rushed in like that, it meant something big had happened.

And her prediction was correct.

"Egostic, Egostic has launched a terror attack! I'll give you the coordinates now!"

"Final... I see. Understood, I'll head out immediately."

Biting her tongue without realizing it, she hardened her expression and stood up. Egostic had returned. After 4 months.

After consciously managing her expression, Stardust was about to open the window and fly out when...

The employee behind her hurriedly tried to provide additional information.

"Oh, and there's something you should know...! Ah..."

However, long before the employee could finish speaking, Stardust had already flown away at lightning speed.

Having ignored the employee's words and suppressing her racing heart, Stardust flew straight to where Egostic had reportedly appeared.

"......?"

She was left speechless at the sight of a massive dragon flying overhead, blocking the sky.

...What is that?
