The coastal terror attack by the global villain organization Lattice affected every country bordering the sea. While many nations still struggled with the aftermath, only South Korea emerged completely unscathed.

The tentative conclusion was that villain Egostic was entirely responsible for South Korea's safety. With this information broadcast across all major networks and the internet, Egostic's recognition and popularity skyrocketed once again. Even people who normally paid no attention to villains now knew who Egostic was.

And now, at the peak of public interest in Egostic, his broadcast finally went live.

"It's been a while. Hello everyone. This is Egostic!"

The camera gradually pulled back, capturing his solitary figure standing in the dark sky.

Egostic began to speak slowly, as if telling a story.

"It's truly a beautiful night. The moon is shining so prettily."

After saying that much, he briefly turned his head with a grin before continuing.

"From ancient times... South Korea has been known to be safe at night. 'No villain can defeat Shadow Walker!' There were many such sayings."

What appeared on screen was the figure of a woman.

"......"

With long purple hair reaching her waist and half-lidded eyes. A beautiful yet mysterious appearance that would make it difficult to approach her first in real life. And contrary to her appearance, she wore what looked like a simple, slightly oversized black hoodie.

About a head shorter than Egostic, she was absentmindedly staring at the purple flame flickering from her small hand.

As if suddenly realizing the camera was focused on her, she turned to face the screen.

"....?"

Still with a languid expression, she tilted her head while looking at the screen. Then she made a V-sign with her fingers and raised it to her face.

"Hmm... is this not right? Well, hello everyone. I'm Mist."

She smiled slightly and raised her hand to chest level, waving it gently.

The chat momentarily froze at the ethereal sight of the beautiful girl with purple hair flowing against the night sky.

In the distance, a dark male figure wrapped in shadows could be seen approaching. The hero Shadow Walker was coming into view.

"Let's see if he can defeat our Mist. I'll be watching from afar with all of you. Now, I'll take my leave!"

With those words, Egostic stepped back.

Before viewers could feel disappointed, Shadow Walker appeared on screen.

"...Villain, what are you doing here."

"Hmm..."

Shadow Walker muttered in a low voice.

In response to his words, the purple-haired woman Mist briefly put her finger to her lips, then answered in a sing-song voice as if stating the obvious.

"Terror?"

And as soon as her words ended.

Whoosh-.

In the dark night sky, a massive ring of purple fire appeared behind her.

Still with half-lidded eyes, she grinned and snapped her fingers.

Multiple purple fireballs materialized around her, all targeting Shadow Walker.

"...Ha. Fighting against Shadow Walker at night. How brave."

"...Well. I think we have to... hmm... try to know what's long and what's short, don't we?"

After responding in a melodic voice, she snapped her fingers once more, and the flames began to blaze toward Shadow Walker.

Shadow Walker also began to conceal himself in shadows to fend off the attack.

The battle had officially begun.

\***

'...Ugh. As expected, my attacks aren't very effective against this one either.'

In the dark night.

Fighting against Mist, the villain Egostic had brought, Shadow Walker lamented inwardly.

Ah! How had he, who once defeated everyone with a single blow, come to this?

Now he faced an increasing number of opponents trying to defeat him.

As the nightmares of the Han Group and the Moonlight Cult resurfaced, bringing a sense of frustration, he gritted his teeth and regained his focus.

'...I see. So this is Egostic's intention.'

Egostic must have known that Shadow Walker would feel powerless against a villain who could largely ignore his abilities.

Yes. Then all this terror was Egostic's stern advice to help him grow mentally. Don't whine, pull yourself together and fight.

"...Right. A man must face any situation without wavering."

He muttered quietly so no one could hear, clenching his fist.

Yes. Who was he? Shadow Walker of South Korea.

He could do this.

With that thought, he looked toward where Egostic was likely watching from afar and made an inner pledge.

Egostic, I will not disappoint you!

"Now, let's get serious."

"Hmm..?"

With renewed determination, he looked at the woman shooting purple fireballs at him.

Yes. He would defeat that woman and earn Egostic's recognition.

With that resolve, Shadow Walker charged once more.

Even to himself, his figure overcoming frustration and leaping forward seemed manly.
