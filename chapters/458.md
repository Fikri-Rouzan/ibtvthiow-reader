"Stardust! This is a real situation. Planes are falling like rain! What on earth should we do?"

"Haha..."

In the sky above the sea.

Whoosh

Against that backdrop, watching planes falling like meteors while engulfed in flames, Stardust let out a hollow laugh.

An apocalyptic scene straight out of the end of the world.

...Could this be Egostic's answer to their previous conversation?

A few months ago.

After she met with Da-in at the cafe, there had been no news of Egostic.

...While Egostic typically had quite a long interval between terrorist acts, Stardust found it strange since she remembered the first three attacks had happened in relatively quick succession.

So she guessed that perhaps her appearance at the cafe that day had given Egostic quite a shock.

In truth, Stardust didn't dislike Egostic at all... she actually felt attracted to him. She had seen through to the truth that he wasn't really a villain.

Da-in probably needed time to think things over too - that's what she thought.

It might have seemed somewhat sudden, but it was necessary at this point. Considering how their relationship would completely flip during the Behemoth incident, what happened that day was absolutely necessary.

...Though it seemed he was not only concerned about that, but also stimulated by Ha-ru's comment about how his terrorist acts were too easy to handle.

"Stardust!!! Where are you! I wonder, even someone like you, can you stop all of this?"

Rumble

The result was dozens of planes falling from the sky.

With fireballs slowly falling one by one from the sky... anyone watching would think the world was ending. In fact, this incident was probably being reported overseas right now.

Certainly in the previous timeline, there had been just one plane, and even a quiz show...

'I created this snowball effect.'

She acknowledged it cleanly.

Discussing the previous timeline was already meaningless. She only wondered how Egostic could carry out a terrorist attack of this scale at this point.

...And so, looking up at the flames falling like meteors in this bright sky that seemed like night.

Stardust could somehow hear Egostic's inner thoughts.

If you're really that strong.

Prove it - that kind of whisper from Egostic.

And just as she was thinking this, right on cue. An association agent rushed up behind her and spoke.

"Ms. Stardust! Huff, puff. I didn't get to tell you earlier, but according to the association's latest investigation, none of those planes have people on board."

"...Is that so?"

"Yes. How they're flying without pilots is still a mystery... but anyway, they're all crashing into the sea, so there shouldn't be any casualties if we leave them be."

"..."

"Egostic probably didn't realize we discovered this point because he rushed the attack. So Ms. Stardust, please don't push yourself..."

As the agent started explaining, somehow Stardust...

"...Haha."

They say people grow similar when they love each other, and laughing just like Da-in... she had a certain thought.

Actually, she could just let this pass. It wouldn't matter if she ignored it. Seeing that there were no people on the planes, even Egostic couldn't guarantee that she could stop them all at this scale.

If she ignored it, it would end as just an incident, but...

She couldn't do that.

'Clearly...'

Because she could understand the feelings with which Da-in had prepared this attack.

'...If you're that strong, try stopping this too. He always gave me trials I could handle to help me grow... now that I've become very strong, is this his way of testing me?'

To help me grow while also confirming my true power.

Stopping this terrorist attack must be his goal.

"...Sigh. Egostic, really..."

Well, if that was the case.

For Stardust, the conclusion was already decided.

After all, she couldn't reject something Egostic had prepared just for her.

"...Still, just in case. I'll try to stop them all."

"What? What are you saying, Ms. Stardust? Stardust? Stardust!!!"

Whoooooosh

After that, Stardust flew up, leaving the agent's voice fading behind her.

'...Starting with the first one falling, to place them all in the sea in order... I need to move faster.'

Whoosh

And so, looking at the red flames approaching before her eyes, Stardust gave a bitter smile.

Somehow, she felt like she'd be in the news for days to come.

As for what comes after... I decided to think about it when the time comes.

After all, my first reason for becoming a villain was to be Stardust's arch-enemy and help her grow stronger when she was weak... but now that she's already strong, that seems somewhat meaningless.

"Let's rest for a few months first... then prepare for the bridge attack."

That's how I concluded my thoughts.

In the end, I need to do what needs to be done. That was my commitment.

However...

"..."
