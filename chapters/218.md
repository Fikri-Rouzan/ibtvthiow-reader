A warm, comfortable energy flowed through our bodies.

"Seriously though, your teleportation is amazing! Like you have a sixth sense or something. I don't think you need any more training?"

"That's right, Da-in. I was watching and it was really incredible."

"You think so?"

I briefly recalled what happened earlier.

Floating in the air, dodging all of Choi Se-hee's lightning bolts.

Roughly speaking, while teleportation consumes a lot of energy, I seem to have good control over it. Makes sense since I've been using it for a while.

Of course, there was the minor issue of feeling dizzy after the sparring session with Choi Se-hee... but Ha-yul's healing helped with that.

"...Still, I should practice teleportation occasionally to maintain the ability. If I don't use it for a long time, I lose the feel for it."

I just need to build up enough stacks to move a certain distance and train.

"True, that's right. That's why I always use my ability periodically."

Choi Se-hee nodded seriously.

...No, I think you just enjoy blasting everything with lightning.

Anyway, teleportation is fine... but what about combat power?

I thought about my other ability, psychokinesis.

Psychokinesis is best used for manipulating guns or weapons. It's a bit weak on its own.

So that leaves Behemoth for physical combat.

I placed my hand on my chest and awakened Behemoth.

Kyuing--?

...Sorry about that. But many of its friends have already fallen from our training, so it should be fine.

"I've been using it all along, but now I'm going to find more ways to utilize it in combat."

Kyuing~

Behemoth, the crystallization of Han Group's knowledge that I retrieved from their underground laboratory.

Usually it doesn't get much use beyond being a bulletproof vest, this unfortunate bio-weapon, but now I need to learn how to control it like my own body.

Because...
