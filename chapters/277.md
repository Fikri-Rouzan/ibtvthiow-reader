The incident known as the Moonlight Catastrophe, when the Moonlight Cult Leader opened gates worldwide to unleash monsters and attack the entire planet, had finally come to an end.

The catastrophe that nearly brought the world to extinction was finally over.

[Everyone! The sun is rising again! Yes, we've won, everyone!!!]

Under the dark night sky, as the gates that had been endlessly pouring out monsters disappeared one by one, people finally realized when they saw the bright sun rising again.

Ah, it's finally over—they thought.

Although numerous cities were destroyed and casualties occurred during the battle, unlike the original timeline where everything collapsed, the world was still intact.

It was a great victory where heroes, villains, civilian ability users, and governments all joined forces to drive away the invaders in an unprecedented global catastrophe.

Although countries worldwide would be busy with post-war measures and rebuilding destroyed cities for a while, experts judged that recovery would be quick since they had survived. With the gates inland all gone and most monsters eliminated, they just needed to focus on recovery efforts.

After the catastrophe ended and things stabilized somewhat, people finally sought to understand how this disaster had been resolved.

And they found a video.

A recording of a broadcast that countless people worldwide had watched live, on the edge of their seats.

[Hello everyone, this is Egostic!]

A villain called Egostic, wearing a black hat and white mask, started broadcasting from South Korea where the Moonlight Cult Leader was located.

Smiling, he said he would stop this incident, and fearlessly headed toward an ominous black tower.

[It's simple. We reversed that gate thing or whatever you created.]

He went to the tower where the cult leader was, manipulated the gate to protect the world from the monster invasion, and finally confronted the Moonlight Cult Leader who caused this catastrophe.

[Well, I did it just fine. Anyway, there's nothing more to do, right?]

[Everyone, today's Egostic surprise event, "Dealing with the Moonlight Cult," ends here. Did you all enjoy it?]

[Now I'll say goodbye for today. Everyone, take care!]

[You...!]

[Bang-]

He finally defeated the Moonlight Cult Leader who caused this whole situation, and as the bright sun rose behind him, dispelling the darkness, the video ended.

And because of all those people who watched the broadcast live, shouting in excitement, and those who saw it later.

South Korea's A-class villain. No, now S-class villain Egostic.
