After the alliance between the Association and Kathedal was formed with the purpose of confronting the Sun God.

The first joint Association-Kathedal meeting.

On the left side of the table sat the Association members in a row, and on the right sat the Kathedal members. At this meeting held in the United States, I spoke while displaying my presentation.

At this gathering attended by the Association Director and Celeste at the helm, along with key heroes including Stardust and major villains like Atlas.

"According to the records I've interpreted from the Sun God's ruins, when the Sun God becomes enraged, angels will descend from the sky and his power will go berserk. This is likely to happen soon."

My newly adopted concept.

Pretending to be a knowledgeable person who knows the world's secrets.

...Actually, it's not a concept but the truth, but since I couldn't say "This world seems like a comic book and I saw it there~," I had to create this persona.

"...Angels descending from the sky. That's hard to believe."

"Unfortunately, it's true. In fact, didn't I already take one angel away?"

"...It was one hundred percent real."

I was already struggling to deal with Stardust and Celeste glaring at each other, and now the Director was giving me trouble.

Anyway, I didn't really care about those suspicious glances.

Because as time passed, the truth would naturally be revealed.

Just as I had predicted.

Murder weapons disguised as angels, with silver hair tinged with gold, dressed in white cloth and wings.

And she was right.

After the angels' attack began.

Essentially, they were South Korea's defense force.

After all, Eun-wol, Choi Se-hee, Seo Ja-young, Divine Dragon, and Uncle Desik were the strongest in our country apart from Stardust.

Well, of course they were—I personally trained them.

This was possible because I knew in advance that the angels would attack, so I had trained and positioned everyone beforehand.

...Of course, there were occasional civil wars among them, but it was cute compared to the original story.

Anyway. Society was being maintained somehow.

Even though angels attacked during the day, and people lived in fear as the D-Day of the prophesied doom drew closer with each passing night. But what does it matter?

Being alive is what counts.

And now that heroes and villains could cooperate, we could witness various unexpected scenes.

"No way. What's wrong with this system?"

"Well... this surveillance program is fairly new..."

"Ugh! How can you boast about using such an ancient relic?"

The S-class villain Seo-eun, wearing a hood with her legs propped up on the chair, occupying the central seat at the Association's Control Center, continuously typing something while looking at the monitor.

Seo-eun, who previously couldn't breach the Association network, was finally able to enter the Association through cooperation and was now controlling South Korea's computer systems like a fish in water.

As a bonus, the Association Control Center could now monitor the entire country in real-time.

Thanks to the special system Seo-eun created, reports were now instantly sent to the Association whenever something happened.

Of course, Su-bin was helping her.

...Anyway, while our country was much better defended than others thanks to Stardust and Ego-stream... other countries were also holding up quite well.

Originally in the story, these angels seemed terrifying because they were deployed as an additional threat to a world already collapsing from various attacks, but now that the whole world was armed specifically to stop them, they weren't that scary.

"Good..."

A week passed like that.

Currently.

Night.

"Yes, yes. Please prepare it that way..."

Night. Night was a wonderful time.

'Come to think of it, does the Sun God even have anything to do with the sun?'

That's just a name people arbitrarily gave him because he resembled the sun, and he liked it so he used it.

...Well, whatever. Maybe the Sun God is just obsessed with his concept.

Especially in our country, with Shadow Walker, a night-exclusive hero, taking care of nighttime security. He's too weak during the day to catch angels, so this is what he should be doing.

Anyway, thanks to this, our scattered Ego-stream members could all gather at home at night.
