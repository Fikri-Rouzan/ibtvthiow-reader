It's out of my hands now. All I can do is cheer them on!

...Egostic finally does another terror attack after so long, and he brings some Japanese woman with him. Holding her hand tightly. Calling her a friend. It was all annoying. She wasn't angry. She was just simply annoyed.

Her smile had long disappeared. All she could see was the dizzying image of Egostic holding hands with that woman.

Without even knowing why herself.

She took a deep breath and thought.

The reason she was angry was because Egostic brought another villain. And a foreign villain to Korea at that? This isn't right. As a hero, she couldn't help but be furious at this situation.

'....So, it's reasonable to deal with the woman in front of me first.'

With that, Stardus reached a cold and rational conclusion. She would crush this villain called Katana and drive her out of Korea.

And then.

...As for what to do with Egostic, hah. Let's think about that later.

".....Then I'll be in your care."

Katana, the woman who had come down to the ground after Egostic had left.

With her black hair tied back flowing in the wind, dressed in a martial arts uniform and drawing her Japanese sword.

Stardus clenched her fist with a serious expression.

As yellow light began to emanate from her fist, Katana leaped toward her like the wind, sword raised.

With a thunderous sound, their clash began in mid-air.

With Stardus's firm resolution.

Even so, if she had no ranged attack means or special abilities,

Katana was confident that she, with her sword, would win.

Back in downtown Seoul.

Moving through the sky at incredible speed, she was pressuring Stardus.

Stardus and Katana. At their level of ability, the fight was no longer like an ordinary battle.

Stardus, still officially A-rank, but practically stronger than most S-rank ability users now.

And Katana, who had gathered many under her with just her swordsmanship and abilities, creating her country's largest villain alliance and crushing the association.

And Katana was also seriously committed to growing her abilities through this opportunity.

Their battle was truly a bloody contest.

'...As expected, the opponent avoids direct confrontation.'

At that moment, Katana.

As she moved through the air at wind-cutting speed, that's what she thought.

Her long, sharp sword, surrounded by sword energy and swung rapidly, made it difficult for Stardus to approach. Especially with Katana's reflexes, she could block Stardus's attacks from any direction with her sword.

While Stardus was fighting with just her fists, if Katana could block all attacks with her sword, the situation was favorable for Katana.

However, unlike Katana who used her abilities to jump high from the ground and then propel herself off building walls to fly around, Stardus could freely fly with her own abilities, which was a difference.

But that didn't necessarily give Stardus an advantage.

"...This is frustrating."

In the sky.

With her hair fluttering, Stardus thought quietly.

It had been several dozen minutes since the fight began, but the situation was ambiguous.

Having to avoid the opponent's sword while trying to attack made landing a hit difficult. In fact, she had only been applying pressure so far without landing a direct hit.

If Stardus were overwhelmingly strong it would be different, but in a situation where their skills were similar, the sword was a dangerous factor. If she tried to attack, she might get cut instead.

So far she was avoiding Katana's attacks while trying to figure out a solution. Avoiding Katana's sword wasn't a problem, but unlike Katana who could freely attack her, it was tricky for her to attack Katana, putting her at a disadvantage.

As Stardus continued fighting while pondering how to avoid the sword and land an attack.

'...Wait, why am I so afraid of that sword?'

Suddenly, a thought flashed through Stardus's mind.

"Phew...."

With her martial arts uniform fluttering, her tied-back hair swaying in the air.

Pat-.

Katana continued to pressure Stardus, looking for an opening.

...Just one hit. Just one successful attack on Stardus would be Katana's victory. The situation was already favorable to her. While Stardus was busy avoiding her sword, an opening would eventually appear. And then she just needed to strike that opening.

Of course, Katana also knew that taking just one hit from Stardus would end it for her too... but who was Katana? She was someone who had taken down thousands single-handedly before. She was an expert at not showing openings.

So Katana continued to fly through the air, looking for her opponent's opening.

And then.

"Hyaaah-!"

Extending a straight fist toward her, Stardus charged at her in a straight line.

And.

'...Found it, an opening.'

Katana calmly collected herself.

She didn't know why her opponent suddenly did this, but this was an opportunity. Deflect that fist with her sword, disrupt her center of gravity, then strike her side. That would end it.

'....Since this isn't a life-or-death battle, I should go easy.'

Making that judgment, Katana prepared to deflect the fist with her sword.

Suddenly, Stardus's fist began emitting an intense light.

'...? What!'

"Hah!"

BOOM.

Changing direction, she aimed her fist not at Katana herself but at the sword itself, striking at an angle.

Before the surprised Katana could do anything, with an intense light, the Japanese sword was shattered.

Clang-!

And so, Katana's sword was shattered into pieces in mid-air.

Katana was shocked.
