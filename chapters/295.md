And Ja-young... was always running away.

...Right. Like they could hear me. Let them enjoy themselves while they can.

Still, seeing Ariel running around so energetically was new to me. In my memory, Ariel was always lying weakly in bed.

"...Well, never mind that."

Anyway, now that I'd gained a powerful ability user, I decided to focus on my work again.

The commotion overseas would die down soon enough. Everyone in Kathedal already knew about my close relationship with Atlas, so it wasn't a big deal. After all, the "real" dangerous villains were all in Kathedal.

That doesn't mean South Korea is safe, though.

"Where the hell are these bastards..."

On this bright, peaceful morning, I, the leader of South Korea's largest villain organization, was... once again contemplating how to protect the country.

The main problems were the Phase 3 villains. How to stop these unique bastards.

Especially since I had completely changed the original storyline by stopping the Moonlight Cult, making it difficult to predict who would appear when.

Of course, some things remained unchanged.

Specifically, the villains who would participate in the Phase 3 final event, the Great Prison Break terrorist attack.

Surprisingly, the Eastern Detention Center, East Carceris, which housed all of South Korea's worst villains, hadn't been affected even when the Moonlight Cult monsters were rampaging. That's to be expected. In the original story, the prison held firm with its impenetrable security even when the Moonlight Gate incident dragged on. With the International Association's technology, I don't understand how they could have failed so spectacularly in the original story.

Anyway, Carceris, the ability user prison built worldwide, and East Carceris in South Korea.

Known as the graveyard for ability users, this place boasted ironclad security, and once you entered, you could never escape. Truly a graveyard for ability users.

Built like a fortress on an artificial island in the East Sea, it housed all the superpowered villains captured so far. Although there weren't many notable ones left since I'd killed most of the worst offenders with the Great Prison Break in mind... still, villains I'd fought before like Crocodileman and Monkey Spanner, as well as more recent additions like Dungeon Maker, were all still locked up there.

And beyond that, ancient villains who had been imprisoned even before the original story began - truly a terrifying place. The fact that it remained safe during the Moonlight Gate incident when all sorts of monsters attacked proved Carceris's incredible security.

...And yet, our original story's Seo-eun had easily neutralized it and freed everyone.

"..."

This was still a mystery to me. In the original story, Seo-eun... the White Witch, had sent warning messages across the country just like I'm doing now, and then suddenly everything was breached at once. How and why this place with ironclad security exploded remains a mystery.

And there was an even bigger problem.

"You're asking if I want to destroy the detention center...? Um, oppa. Why would you think I'd want to do that...?"

When I asked if she ever felt the urge to destroy that prison, Seo-eun had answered with a look of disbelief.

But more importantly, after looking into it briefly, she had shaken her head and replied:

"Um... and oppa, after looking into this, it seems like a prison break would be really difficult?"

"...Huh?"

"Their security has its own separate network like the Association, so it wouldn't be easy to breach with my technical skills. It might be possible if I devoted years to it, but even then..."

"I see..."

Hearing Seo-eun's explanation, I was shocked.

Wait, then how did the original Seo-eun breach it? Especially since she wasn't with us back then and had to teach herself, so her abilities would have been weaker than now...?

'...She must have had help.'

After pondering this, I reached a conclusion.

Yes, that must be it. Though it's just speculation... considering the original story, it's possible.

I tried to recall as much as I could and got back to work.

Especially to prevent "that thing" inside the detention center from getting out, which I'd been researching for a while.

Even without a mass breakout like in the original story... that event could still happen.

Of course, the work wasn't easy, but...

'Right. I need to prepare everything in advance.'

All for Stardust, to help her move forward as she carries the fate of this world on her shoulders.

She must be working hard too, so I can't slack off.
