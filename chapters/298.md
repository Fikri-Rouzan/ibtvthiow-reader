\# A Phone Call with Lee Seol-ah

After listening to everything I had to say, she sighed and said she understood. We agreed to talk more later, and I ended the call.

"I'll try my best... but like you said, Da-in, sneaking into the detention center will probably be difficult."

That's what she told me.

However.

"Still, if you're determined to get yourself captured, I think I can arrange for you to be placed in the deepest level of Carceris during the summary trial process."

She said that much she could do for me. I thanked her for her help. Before we hung up, she added:

"And please be careful. Take care of yourself. You're always making people worry."

"Haha. Sorry, but this is the only way, isn't it?"

"...Fine. I'll look into it more on my end."

After ending the call, I sat with a pen in hand, deep in thought.

The Wish Granter. It would be best to eliminate him before Phase 4 begins, but...

"...Is this really the right time to do this?"

Yes, that's what I was contemplating.

The main event related to him occurs in the final chapter of Phase 3. But since we're still in the early part of Phase 3, the timing is quite ambiguous. In other words, is it right to eliminate him already, contrary to the original story?

"Well. When have I ever followed the original story anyway?"

...Of course, it seemed ridiculous to quickly eliminate the highlight event of Phase 3's final chapter so early, but I had no choice. If I don't take care of it now and things go wrong later, there would be no solution.

So I reviewed my plan once more.

Get captured by Stardust, enter Carceris, and defeat the Wish Granter, Bottom Influence. Theoretically, a beautiful plan.

And since he's one of the final bosses of Phase 3...

Just as I've always prepared in advance to stop the Han-eun Group and the Moonlight Cult...

I've been preparing for this one as well.

"....Sigh."

The Wish Granter.

A being who grants wishes according to the value of what you sacrifice—what's most precious to you. However, in a truly horrific way.

As one of the Sun God's creations, he's an extremely bizarre creature. From the description of a shadow-covered black entity the size of a 5-year-old child sitting among thick white tentacles, he clearly isn't human. He seems to exist in a different dimension from us, making him extremely difficult to deal with.

Because of this, I had already thought of a way to defeat him early on when I first arrived in this world.

And what I prepared was...

This diary that I've been writing in regularly.

"...Well then."

I muttered as I pulled the diary from the bookshelf and opened it.

Inside were detailed accounts of everything I'd done since the first day I arrived in this world.

...Since it contained a lot of personal content—including detailed explanations about the original story—I had asked the Vine Witch to seal it so that only I could open it.

And finally, the time had come to use it.

"...I guess I won't be writing in this much longer."

I muttered after skimming through the diary.

Just reading through it briefly brought back vivid memories of everything I'd done so far. Yes, this should be enough.

So that's taken care of.

Now the remaining question is how to get captured and enter Carceris.

"...I'll have to commit an act of terrorism. It's been a while."

I twirled my pen as I muttered.

Yes, terrorism. Come to think of it, it's been quite some time since I last did anything like that. When was the last time? Actually, I haven't done anything since the Moonlight Cult incident, and before that, the last time was when I launched missiles to train Stardust.

At that thought, I paused.

'...Wait.'

For a villain, I haven't committed any acts of terrorism for several months, and instead have been doing strange things like stopping the Moonlight Cult and protecting Stardust from the Dungeon Maker...?

Isn't this dangerous?

After reading that far, I realized this could become a serious problem if it continued.

The reason I can still be treated as a villain regardless of what I do is because of terrorism. Just like someone who has committed murder will always be considered a murderer, I maintain my identity as a villain because of terrorism. No matter what anyone says, he's a bad villain who commits acts of terrorism! Like that.

So now is the time, in many ways, to commit an act of terrorism. Actually, just destroying one building would constitute terrorism, so it's not a big problem...

This is the perfect opportunity to commit one big act of terrorism and then get captured by Stardust and go to prison.

"Oh, oppa!"

"Seo-eun, I have something to tell you."

"What is it?"

"Let's commit an act of terrorism."

"Really? Finally!"

Seo-eun was delighted at my words.

"Oh, and this time, I'm planning to get captured by Stardust on purpose."
