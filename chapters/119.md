Deep in a mountain range where no one can find it.

Standing proudly in the center is a large house.

This is the Ego-stream headquarters.

Beyond being South Korea's greatest villain alliance, it's now becoming Korea's premier gathering of supernatural ability users.

And I, the founder and leader of this alliance,

was currently busy comforting a crying Seo-eun.

"Sniff...."

"It's okay, it's okay."

After being unconscious for a night, she woke up, sat blankly on the bed, and suddenly started crying.

I was startled when she began crying right after waking up, so I was frantically trying to comfort her. Seriously, what's making you so sad?

"Sniff.... I could have won..."

Ah, so that's what she's upset about.

...But Seo-eun, um. It didn't really look like you could win...?

Still sniffling in my arms, Seo-eun began to apologize to me.

"I'm sorry, oppa... If I had just done a little better, I could have won..."

"No. It's okay, it's okay, and you didn't necessarily have to win."

"Sob sob sob..."

As I held the crying Seo-eun in my arms to comfort her, I quietly thought.

Actually, Seo-eun... this was a fight you weren't supposed to win from the beginning...

Since Ego-stream itself is an organization planned to periodically give trials to Stardust, it would be somewhat troublesome if we actually defeated Stardust.

...Or would it? Maybe losing would make her work harder?

While I was thinking about this and stroking Seo-eun's hair, Soo-bin and Choi Se-hee's faces peeked through the doorway behind Seo-eun's back.

They probably came after hearing the crying.

Choi Se-hee silently mouthed 'Can I come in?' to me, so I gave a slight nod.

Yes. Please help me comfort her.

After entering the room, Choi Se-hee spoke to Seo-eun from beside her.

"Hey, Seo-eun."

"Eek?!"

Seo-eun was startled when she suddenly heard Choi Se-hee's voice from beside her.

She quickly pulled away from my arms and said:

"Oh, u-unni?"

"Yes. I'm your unni. Stop crying and look at this."

Choi Se-hee handed over her phone as she said that.

Seo-eun, with her eyes slightly red from crying, took it and read the posts displayed on the screen.

".....Unni, what is this?"

"What else? These are posts saying how cute you were during the terror attack. How about it, feeling better?"

"...Ha. Unni, I'm not an elementary school kid who suddenly feels better seeing things like this. I'm not the type to care what others think."

Seo-eun. That statement would have been more convincing if you hadn't been smiling while saying it.

Seo-eun spent a long time looking at the phone. Even other posts. She was looking so intently that Choi Se-hee, who was standing in front of her, said, 'Um... should I take it back now?'

After thoroughly reading the posts on my fan cafe, Seo-eun, who had been crying just moments ago, suddenly seemed energized as she clenched her fist and said:

"....That's right! This terror attack wasn't my only one, and I have more in the future, so I can win next time!"

Suddenly speaking with confidence, Seo-eun then quietly and solemnly whispered to me while still clenching her fist:

"....Oppa, my Star Buster 2.0 will be different from last time. I'll make sure of it."

"Uh... yeah. Fighting?"

"Great! Unni!!!"

"U-um?"

"Follow me to the basement! From today, we're making a new Star Buster!"

"Hey, wait a minute!"

Choi Se-hee hurriedly chased after Seo-eun, who had suddenly jumped out of bed full of enthusiasm.

"......"

At least she's got her spirit back, so I guess that's good...?

"Wow... no way, there's so little of this?"

Egostic_highlights, Egostic_mad_movie, K-villain Egostic that shocked the world, things like that.
