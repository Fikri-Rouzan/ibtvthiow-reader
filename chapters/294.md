Chapter 294 - Dash

Above the sea near Busan.

Inside a wall made of giant water droplets.

Stardust was glaring uncomfortably at Egostic, who was saying he would take Ariel with him.

She had come down after hearing that the villain organization Latis suddenly launched a terror attack on Busan.

And the truth of the terror that she discovered was quite simple.

...That woman called Ariel wanted to be with Egostic. She did it to get Egostic's attention.

From what she heard, those two knew each other from the beginning, and it seems that Egostic had refused when the woman called Ariel asked to join his villain organization, Egostream. And the result of that was this woman's terror attack.

So.

To summarize in one sentence, another woman had appeared wanting to be by Egostic's side.

And this situation.

"...You two know each other? More importantly, home?"

Stardust was very displeased with this.

With her arms crossed, she spoke in a cold voice.

The fact that another woman would be added to Egostic's side. Why did the fact that he would be living at home with another woman make her uncomfortable? Surely she and Egostic weren't anything to each other...

'...No, that's not right.'

As she thought this, she changed her mind.

They weren't nothing to each other. He and she were bound by the relationship of hero and villain. The relationship of being each other's nemesis.

And didn't he say it? That she was important to him...

So.

It's not strange at all to feel this way.

And when a villain is trying to recruit a new villain, of course she can be angry as a hero.

...In truth, she wasn't sure if that was the only reason.

Anyway, with such heated feelings, Stardust was about to open her mouth with a still cold face toward the flustered Egostic when at that moment.

"Miss Stardust."

Suddenly.

Egostic approached her.

"...Yes?"

In mid-air, he suddenly approached her.

...What is this? Before she could even question it, he was striding toward her without hesitation, and Stardust was flustered.

And before Stardust could continue her thoughts, Egostic was standing right in front of her.

"...What, what is it?"

Though she looked up at Egostic with her arms crossed and a deliberately cold face, inside she was extremely flustered.

And whether he knew Stardust's feelings or not.

Standing close enough that their breaths touched, Egostic looked into Stardust's eyes and said.

"Miss Stardust. You know that I cherish you more than anyone else, right?"

"...Huh?"

Both soaked from being caught in the rain on their way to the sea.

With his hair slightly shining with moisture as if he had just washed it, Egostic smiled quietly and said.

"Watching you always put others first with your kindness, I have never for a moment thought you were anything less than amazing. And you're more beautiful than anyone else."

"...Wait, what are you saying?"

"Actually... that's why, although I didn't want to do it here, I have something I want to tell you, Miss Stardust."

Egostic's sudden words.

Caught in this unexpected turn of atmosphere, she forgot her anger about Ariel and was so confused by the current situation that her head was spinning. No, what is he saying right now? There are people watching.

Indeed, Ariel with blue hair was watching them from a distance, her face red with embarrassment as she covered it.

"...I, I'm fine being second!"

What is she saying now?

As Stardust was flustered with her own face turning red at this sudden turn of events.

Suddenly Egostic grabbed her hand.

"Miss Stardust."

"Uh, yes?"

As Stardust, with her hand held, looked at Egostic's serious face, in that moment when she couldn't grasp the situation and didn't even think to catch Egostic.

"I'm sorry."

"...Huh?"

With those words.

The image of Egostic in front of her suddenly flashed and disappeared.

Then he appeared behind Ariel, and disappeared again. With her.

With that, the water structure that had formed the place lost its power and vanished.

Swoosh.

"......."

Left alone, Stardust quietly looked at her hand and muttered.

"...Huh?"

That's right.

Egostic had fled like that.

The terror attack ended without casualties.

...Leaving only Stardust, who couldn't grasp the situation.
