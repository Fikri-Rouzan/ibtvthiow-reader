\# Brazil's Downfall

From this point in the original story, the world gradually begins to spiral into chaos.

The number of supernatural ability users, which was once quite limited, continues to grow, and with that comes an increase in villains. These groups form alliances and even invade foreign countries, causing widespread turmoil.

Until now, incidents involving Behemoth, Han-eun Group, and the Moonlight Cult only devastated Korea, but now everyone globally will start suffering together. Of course, our country will naturally suffer twice as much.

And obviously, I have no intention of watching that happen.

Even if I lack the information to cover overseas territories, shouldn't I be able to at least protect a small peninsula like Korea if I put my all into it?

Anyway, the international community will gradually become more chaotic.

To survive in this war, military power is obviously necessary.

And since this world relies on supernatural ability users for all military power, human resources are ultimately the most important.

In other words, the crucial battle is about which faction has even one more talented individual.

"....."

Seo-eun, the ultimate hacker and inventor; Death Knight, an undying war machine; and Eun-wol, who utilizes magic—a concept different from supernatural abilities. I've gathered them all before they could fall into corruption as they did in the original story.

I've even gone all out to pre-emptively recruit several villains who would appear later. The precious elemental ability user Choi Se-hee and the healing ability user Lee Ha-yul, who becomes increasingly important later on, are prime examples.

Of course, these individuals alone are already quite powerful, but you can never have too much talent. Especially considering the latter part of the original story... I'm still hungry.

But here's the thing.

As the scale continues to grow, other factors grow alongside talent.

In the end, whether villain or hero, it's all about people dealing with people. Sure, monsters appear later on, but until then, people are behind all the terrorism.

So politics becomes somewhat necessary.

And since factions are essential for politics, that's also why I created the villain alliance.

"....It must be soon."

The world map on the wall.

Looking at it intently, I quietly muttered.

After Brazil's downfall, society was initially chaotic but eventually stabilized again. However, I know this stability won't last long.

If things follow the original story, the next event will happen soon.

The first worldwide terrorist attack and the largest terror incident on Earth to date: the coastal invasion by the villain organization "Latis."

And.
