Come to think of it, this world is truly a mess.

I looked down at the city through the window and had that thought.

On the surface, it looks normal. Countless people moving busily between towering buildings. From this view, it seems no different from the world of my previous life.

However, reality tells a different story. And it's mostly a mess.

First of all, people who awaken superpowers are born every now and then. But it's not like these powers are manageable. The problem is that they're all absurdly strong.

Stardus alone could probably level dozens of buildings if she felt like it. The same goes for others. It's even more of a disaster overseas. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say that almost every country faces a crisis of destruction every month.

Of course, perhaps thanks to the world's balance, heroes stronger than most villains appear, so things are somehow managing to stay afloat. In other words, without heroes, nations wouldn't even function properly. Because of that, the government's influence is significantly diminished compared to the Association. The president is also a bit paranoid.

And are villains the only problem? That's not all. Looking at the Haneun Group, large corporations are also just out of their minds. Secret experiments are a given, and their rival, Yuseong Enterprise, is a mastermind behind the scenes, pressuring the government and the Association to control South Korea. Since the Haneun Group collapsed, they've probably been trying to swallow South Korea in earnest. Well, for me, it's better that way. That way, I only have to control Lee Seol-ah.

Anyway, my point is this. This world is not normal. Superpowered individuals cause terrorism left and right, the government is useless, corporations pull the strings, and there are even gods and an underworld—it's a total mess. I miss the peaceful world I used to know.

"...."

I continued to stare out the window, lost in thought.

What's even funnier? This level is actually quite peaceful.

From now on, stronger villains will emerge, and the world will become even more chaotic.

And Stardus will have to handle all of that alone.

"*Oppa*, what on earth are you looking at all by yourself so gloomily?"

"...It's nothing. Let's go now."

Awakened from my thoughts by Seo-eun's voice, I turned my head.

Yeah.

In the end, I'm the one who has to stop all of this.

South Korea's top conglomerate was plotting to rule South Korea through force behind the scenes while maintaining a kind image externally.

They were secretly conducting illegal and inhumane human experiments and creating monsters, but all their conspiracies were exposed through the Behemoth incident.

As a result, the company collapsed, and high-ranking executives, including the chairman, fled overseas, but the researchers who conducted the experiments, led by Dr. Kim Seonwoo, could not escape.

In the original work, Kim Seonwoo even tried to fight Stardus by seizing Behemoth, but he eventually lost and fled.

Eventually, the employees led by him realized they were finished, and since they were all going to die anyway, they planned to go out with a *bang* and take everyone with them.

Somewhere deep in the provinces.

Haneun Group Secret Base, Sector C.

At the very center of an underground space so deep you couldn't see the bottom, countless people were moving about frantically.

They were all skin and bones, bodies worn down to the extreme, and looked filthy as if they hadn't even washed properly.

But their eyes alone burned fiercely, filled with sharp, poisonous intensity.

Among the figures in white lab coats rushing about, one man was cutting through the crowd, heading somewhere.

He looked like the oldest among them.

He also looked the most exhausted.

Yet his eyes burned the fiercest of all, blazing with a vicious glare that looked like it could tear anyone apart.

He was the overall director of Haneun Group's Behemoth Project.

Abandoned by the board of directors and left behind in Korea.

Doctor Kim Seonwoo.

To the researcher hurriedly following at his side, he spat his words like he was chewing them up.

"So. You're saying even the final inspection is done?"

"Yes, Doctor. All that's left is deployment."

"I'll see it myself."

The place he arrived at after walking step by step was—

An empty cavernous hall of overwhelming scale, like an entire massive city had been turned on its side and shoved underground.

And filling that space completely.

Its vertical length was several times that of a skyscraper, its width like a vast mountain range.

Haneun Group's ultimate masterpiece, forged by grinding down their very last spark of life, created solely for destruction, collapse, and chaos.

A weapon made for annihilation, Project Octopass.

"So... it's finally done. Finally."

Clenching his teeth, Kim Seonwoo muttered under his breath.

Then, unable to contain his excitement, he suddenly shouted toward all the staff.

"All personnel, listen up!!!"

A destructive volume that filled the entire vast hall.

As a nearby researcher hurriedly set up a microphone so his voice would broadcast throughout the entire facility.

His voice echoed through the underground.

"How much have we sacrificed for Haneun Group until now, and for the development of this nation!"

"And the citizens who criticize and slander us, who devoted ourselves solely to a better world! What have they ever done for this country!"

"How much have they oppressed us, who tried to recreate human history through evolution, and now they even try to purge us!"

"A rotten country like this is no longer necessary!"

"Now, isn't it our final calling to turn this hateful country into a sea of fire with our own hands!"

"Tomorrow, we—!"

He spat out the final words with eyes blazing.

"Will destroy Seoul."

As his speech ended, cheers erupted all around. Agreement. A sea of fire. Destruction. Rage.

Amid all that deafening noise, he quietly closed his eyes.

Yes. Tomorrow.

Seoul would finally learn what the end truly meant, in the palm of his hand.
