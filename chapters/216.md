Stardust's attention was elsewhere.

The two embracing like lovers.

But even if her head understood, her heart felt differently.

Stardust's heart raced as she watched them like that.

'...Why am I feeling this way?'

Logically speaking, it shouldn't matter what they did. A villain embracing someone shouldn't be important to her.

And yet. And yet.

Why?

Why did her chest hurt as if someone was stabbing it?

Her eyes were fixed on their hands, clasped together since earlier.

"......"

"Then we'll conclude today's terror here. Take care, everyone!"

Still smiling, he manipulated the camera slightly before turning his head to look at Stardust again.

Still holding Katana's hand.

"Haha, Stardust. Next time I won't lose...."

As he spoke to her, he suddenly paused when he saw her expression.

...Honestly.

Stardust didn't even know why she was angry.

What did it matter what Egostic did? They were just villain and hero, nothing more, nothing less.

But.

The sight of Egostic firmly holding Katana's hand.

Perhaps it reminded her unconsciously of some forgotten scene from her memory.

Deep in her subconscious, in a place she couldn't recognize.

The thought that it should be her hand he was holding. A sense of loss, as if something had been taken from her.

For some reason she couldn't quite understand,

Stardust, Shin Ha-ru, couldn't stop her eyes from growing hot with emotion.

"S-Stardust...?"

Seeing her sudden strange behavior, Egostic looked confused.

...Yet still holding Katana's hand, which made Stardust feel even more upset.

She felt like they had made some kind of promise that he had broken, making her resent him.

Stardust, with her fists clenched tightly,

Snapped at him.

"...You, jerk."

...That was the best she could do.

Even Ha-ru herself couldn't explain what exactly was wrong,

But Egostic was definitely a jerk.

She raised her head again.

Her blue eyes glistening in the light, reflecting... moisture?

".....Jerk."

"....."

As both Katana and I remained silent,

She quickly wiped her eyes with her hands.

"......"

She glared at me once more,

Then turned away, showing me her back.

Whoooosh.

She disappeared as fast as the wind.

And.
