\# Death Knight

For hundreds of years, he had been trapped inside a narrow ring, sleeping. Now, he was thoroughly enjoying his newfound freedom.

And fortunately, thanks to finding a good ring master.

[Kuhahahahaha!]

He was, after a long time, engaged in a proper battle.

"Haa... Haa..."

The woman fighting before him, breathing heavily, was Stardust—the person Egostic had told him to face.

'Fight with some restraint, and when it progresses to a certain point, pretend to lose and run. Got it?'

Recalling Egostic's instructions, he gripped his spear again.

This battle after so long was truly enjoyable. Though he was somewhat limiting his power, the woman before him was doing the same. This allowed for a balanced fight to continue.

As the battle progressed considerably, when they were momentarily separated after being thrown apart, he honestly shared his impression with Stardust.

[Kuhahahahaha! Egostic was right. You are quite strong. To match me evenly! It's been nearly hundreds of years since I faced such an opponent.]

It was actually true.

Even though he was controlling his power to some extent, countless humans couldn't even withstand this much.

In other words, this woman who had made him use this much power was quite strong.

After he delivered those words with respect, the blonde woman in front of him narrowed her eyes and asked him something else.

Specifically, where Egostic was.

Death Knight was puzzled by this sudden question, but then recalled what Egostic had told him.

'Stardust might ask where I am, you know? Since this is being broadcast on my stream. She came to catch me, but you're there instead. So... right. Just say, "I'm your opponent. Egostic won't be coming." Just say that.'

Yes. He definitely said that.

Remembering his instructions, Death Knight confidently declared in a loud voice, with a bit of provocation mixed in.

[Egostic won't be coming today! He said someone like me would be enough to handle you! Kuhahahahahahaha!]

And at that moment.

The atmosphere around the hero called Stardust suddenly began to change.

[......!]

A rapidly changing atmosphere that only someone who had faced countless battlefields could sense.

From her, who until now had been fighting at a moderate level.

Suddenly, an immense fighting spirit began to pour out. Something deep and viscous.

The air swirled around her, and her hair began to float, showing that the situation was turning ominous.

And in such a situation.

Death Knight, instead, laughed loudly.

[Kuhahahahaha! Good! Yes, that's how it should be!]

From her changed atmosphere, he realized that from now on, this woman called Stardust would also unleash her full power.

Though he couldn't quite understand why she suddenly changed, if it meant a more intense fight, that was all good.

Good. The real battle starts now.

Now I, Death Knight, must fight seriously too. No more holding back.

With that determination, Death Knight beat his chest with one arm and shouted at her.

[Yes, the true match begins now! Hero Stardust, come! My full power...]

And as he was saying that.

In the blink of an eye, Stardust flew in front of him.

With her blonde hair fluttering, and a fist clenched in one hand.

Ah. I let my guard down.

As Death Knight hurriedly tried to block her with his spear,

"Too late."

Such a brief murmur brushed past him.

And simultaneously, an enormous impact was felt in his stomach.

Kwaaaang.

His body was thrown backward at tremendous speed.

\***

"......."

Stardust, dusting off her fist, rose into the sky. To head toward where she had sent that Death Knight flying.

[Cough, cough. Kuuuuu....]

The one sent flying by her punch was embedded in the outer wall of a building on the opposite side, coughing up black smoke.

[Kuuu. To think that I, would let my guard down... cough, cough. I admit it. It's my defeat.]

With his armor drooping from his arms and legs, he collapsed.

Sent flying and left in a critical state by her single punch after her sudden awakening, he still muttered with a smile despite his shattered body.

Then, as he gradually began to turn to dust.

Even in such a situation, Death Knight delivered his final lines to her who was looking down at him.

[This isn't my end. After all, I have an immortal body. When I return next time, I won't let my guard down, so be prepared. Kuhuhuhuhuh...]

After leaving those final words, even his last helmet turned to dust and was blown away by the wind.

And in the half-destroyed urban street after the fierce battle, now empty as everyone had fled, only Stardust stood alone.

Looking at where Death Knight had finally disappeared, Stardust raised her head to look at the sky.

Egostic still hadn't come.
