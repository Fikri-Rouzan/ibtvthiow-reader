Slap.

When I smacked the back of his head, the crocodile bastard let out a grunt.

"Graaaah!!!!"

He swung his arm backward in a wide arc, trying to hit me.

But of course, I'd already teleported far away.

Well, he's not listening at all.

Seriously, what did I even do to make him this hostile?

Aren't villains supposed to have some kind of internal camaraderie with each other? Like, even if we've never met, we should join forces against our common enemy—heroes, right? That's the kind of dynamic I was hoping for. Why is he just charging at me like a brute?

Actually, I was planning to backstab him the moment he let his guard down, so maybe if he sensed that, he's not so dumb after all?

But watching him charge at me again like a raging bull, I concluded he really was just an idiot. Does this guy seriously have zero learning ability?

I teleported away again just before he could tackle me. Hah, hah. Truth is, I'm starting to hit my limit too. I've been teleporting back and forth from here to my house—do you know how exhausting that is? My stamina was already lacking, and all this zipping around is slowly wearing me down. Plus, I'm holding a microphone in one hand and carrying a bundle on my back like Santa Claus.

But a true professional never shows weakness. I grinned as if nothing was wrong and called out to him.

"Hey buddy, are we gonna do this all day?"

"Graaaaaaah!!!! You coward!!!!!!!"

The trick is to brazen it out—only then can I win.

I read it years ago in some self-help book:

Life is like iron-plate fried rice.

Rice stir-fried on a scalding iron plate tastes amazing.

Honestly, slap anything on that hot metal and it turns out decent.

Life works the same way.

Live life on an iron plate and, more often than not, things go smoothly.

"So what!! I never asked!!!!"

Wait, isn't that his special move?

The instant whatever-it-is shoots from his mouth, I teleport to the side. Lunatic!

In my haste I drop the mic—first and last time I've ever used the thing outside of singing in my room. Great, now it's toast.

I reappear a hair's breadth away and see a jet of water carving the spot I'd stood.

That's Crocodile Man's finisher, Extreme Water Spouting.

He named it himself in the original; in reality it's just a water cannon. One direct hit and you're dead.

No idea what crocodiles have to do with water cannons, but in the canon Stardus took one out of nowhere and nearly died. I already knew, so I barely dodged.

"You rat bastard!!!! You actually dodged that!!!!"

He wipes his mouth, snarling.

He just used an instant-death attack on me?

If I hadn't seen it coming I'd be dead right now.

Fury flares in my chest.

Who does he think he is, pulling this in a safe zone?

Screw it.

I was only going to scare him and finish him later, but...

Let's wrap this up tonight.

Tonight's dinner: crocodile meat.
