"Urgh...."

In a wide street where people had already fled in all directions, leaving no one behind, Shin Ha-ru was engaged in battle with a giant robot known as "Starbuster."

[Haa, haa. Huff, I was a bit worried when my brother said you were so strong, but you're not that strong after all!]

The girl inside Starbuster was trying her best to taunt despite looking slightly tired.

Dodging another steel fist flying toward her, Shin Ha-ru thought to herself.

'Where on earth does Egostic keep finding these villains?'

No, not just one or two, but like peeling an onion, new villains kept appearing one after another, making her wonder. It hadn't even been a few years since Egostic said he would create a villain alliance, and already he had introduced quite a few villains. Electra, Team Lapis, Death Knight, and now this.

And now there was this woman piloting some kind of walking armored vehicle that was as sturdy as a tank.

[Haah. Try attacking! Why do you keep running away?]

...And this girl looked strangely young no matter how you looked at her. Her age was roughly... hard to tell, maybe middle or high school?

Having made this assessment, Shin Ha-ru was filled with anger again.

Employing a minor to do villain work—was Egostic in his right mind?

[Haah, ha! Seeing how you only run away, you're nothing special, lady!]

...But such thoughts gradually faded with the continued taunting.

The constant provocations were starting to crack Shin Ha-ru's mental fortitude.

While she had been thinking 'This poor child is being used by Egostic!' until just a moment ago, now her thoughts had changed to giving this impudent kid a proper adult education.

...Lady? Ha. What kind of baseless provocation was that?

How old did she think she was? Ha, ridiculous.

This childish taunt, which she had never heard from any villain before, actually filled her with rage.

But letting emotions get the better of you before completing the job was amateur behavior.

'A first-class hero must kill their emotions and fight with sharp reason.'

"......"

That saying she had heard somewhere in the past.

Recalling that memory, she cooled her head and analyzed the situation again.

Boom-. Boom-.

Currently, the robot kept attacking, and she was either dodging or occasionally blocking while fighting defensively.

And the girl inside that machine.

[Ugh, aah! Stop just dodging and fight back!]

"......"

She was getting annoyed at Stardust who kept running away and only blocking her attacks.

[Are you going to dodge this too? How about this? Hya!]

Stardust responded defensively to observe the situation.

Watching her, the girl inside the machine muttered.

[I don't get it. Why does my brother always care only about you?]

".....!"

Shin Ha-ru froze at those words.

....He only cares about me?

Trying to control her suddenly racing heart, Shin Ha-ru's mind raced.

In truth, there wasn't much information about Egostic. He was a mysterious figure who always said what he wanted to say and then disappeared.

She dodged another incoming attack and glanced toward where Egostic was.

He was sitting on one of the floating metal cages, talking to the camera.

Yes. This might be her chance.

A chance to obtain information about Egostic, more valuable than anything else.

...A chance to learn more about him, something she had always been curious about.

Having made this decision, Stardust briefly flew away to create distance from the Starbuster, then shouted at her in an angry voice.

"You. Just what kind of villain are you? Another one newly hired by Egostic?"

Pretending to be angry, she mixed in some provocation to gather information.

If she had been facing Electra or Death Knight, she would have only received answers like 'Hmm... who knows?' or 'Hahaha!' and that would have been the end of it.

[What did you say! I wasn't newly hired, I've been his comrade from the very beginning!]

Bingo.

As expected, the girl in the robot started spilling information.

It was a bit annoying how she emphasized being a comrade from the beginning, or called him "brother" to seem close... but this could be used to her advantage.

Still maintaining her outward anger, Shin Ha-ru asked again, with the most incredulous voice in the world.

"What nonsense are you talking about? Everyone already knows you're not Egostic's first comrade. If you were his comrade, what have you been doing all this time?"

[Ugh....!]

Hearing her provocation, the girl burst with frustration and began operating the robot more roughly.

The attacks came much stronger than before.

Dodging those punches, Stardust focused intently on what the girl was saying.

[I am the first comrade! I'm the one who's been doing all the broadcast hijacking and hacking from behind the scenes! It's so unfair!]

The girl spoke as if she was truly wronged.

Hearing this, Stardust raised her voice slightly in surprise and asked.

"You did all that hacking? From where exactly?"

[From where? Obviously, from the same house...]

The girl who had been excitedly talking suddenly stopped mid-sentence.

Then, she whispered so quietly it was barely audible.

'...Huh? No, brother. Isn't this much okay? No? ... Alright, hmph.'

After whispering as if talking to someone inaudibly, she shouted loudly again.

[...Stop saying useless things and just fight properly!]

After saying that, she kept her mouth firmly shut and focused on the battle.

'....Tch.'

Clicking her tongue inwardly while dodging another attack, she looked back and saw Egostic looking in her direction.

...She had almost gotten everything, what a shame.

But it didn't matter.

She could hear the rest of the story after taking her to the Association.

She then stopped her passive behavior of just dodging and began to summon strength back into her body.

Her aura suddenly changed.

And simultaneously.

She charged at Starbuster again. Fiercely.

[...Huh? Wait!]

End this quickly.

That was her only thought.
