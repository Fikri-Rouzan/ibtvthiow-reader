\# Sun God Combat Control Tower

Naturally, after the situation was assessed, what everyone became interested in was:

"Wow..."

The battle between the Sun God and Stardust.

For more detailed battle information, we could watch the footage from thousands of drones that continued filming even as they were destroyed.

"....."

I stood by the window, watching the holographic display of Stardust and the Sun God's battle.

Several minutes into the fight already.

Most people were watching in awe as Stardust, in a human body, fought equally against a god.

But I knew the truth.

Stardust had a divine nature within her, making this essentially a battle between gods.

And.

"...Ha."

I knew that in the original story, Stardust had defeated the Sun God.

'And now Stardust is...'

Thanks to my efforts, much stronger than in the original story.

In other words, this was a battle that couldn't possibly be lost.

So I watched calmly with inner reassurance, though not letting my guard down completely. I observed the battle with composure.

There was no more room for me to intervene in this fight. This was purely divine power against divine power.

'...'

I watched without blinking the battle that would determine everything.

clenched her golden glowing fist again with gritted teeth.

"Do you have time to think about other things?"

"Kuhk...!"

That it wasn't going to be easy.

...No matter how much divine power Stardust had received, her body was still human.

Although the star's power negated most of the attack, the Sun God was still stronger than any opponent she had ever faced.

So currently, Stardust was barely managing to block Helios's attacks.

PUEEEENG. PUEEEENG. PUEEEENG.

...Was victory really possible?

'No, Ms. Stardust. You can do it.'

Stardust unconsciously strengthened her weakening legs.

Yes. Egostic had said she could defeat the Sun God.

If she collapsed here... what would happen to Egostic, her friends, and this world?

That thought made Stardust's heart burn hot again.

And her will made the star's power within her blaze anew.

The pure power of the Sun God pounding her body.

Stardust. Her manifestation of the star's divine nature.
