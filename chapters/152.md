A few days later.

I was sitting across from Lee Seol-ah on the top floor of Yu-seong Corporation.

Though she still looked haggard and was leaning back in her chair as if tired, she was smiling slightly.

"Did something good happen? You seem happy."

"Ah. Yes, there is. I've finally managed to take control of almost all the major domestic companies."

"Oh, really?"

"Yes. VK Corporation was the most difficult, but I just had their president arrested on corruption charges that Da-in provided. Once I finish wrapping things up, I can focus all my efforts on the political sphere."

She smiled proudly despite looking exhausted.

...As expected, even though she's slowed down a bit, Lee Seol-ah is steadily devouring the Republic of Korea according to the original story.

Personally, the more her influence grows, the better it is for me, so I praised her. As we enter the latter part of the original story, having everything unified under one person will be much better for overcoming crises. It means I can intervene in everything.

After enjoying my praise for a moment, her face darkened slightly, and then she forced a smile and said to me.

"...Ah, right. I also heard about Da-in."

"Huh? What about me?"

"I heard you're dating someone..."

As she trailed off ambiguously, I waved my hand in denial.

"Oh, that. No, it's not true. She was just joking around, we're not in any kind of relationship."

"...Really?"

"Of course. When would I even have time to date someone?"

Hearing my denial, she sighed with apparent relief and answered.

"...That's good thinking. Romance only reduces work efficiency and is an inefficient waste of time, so I don't think there's any need for it either."

...Hmm. I didn't go that far.

Anyway, I responded seriously to Lee Seol-ah, who was speaking to me very earnestly.

"Right. Anyway, you know why I came here today, right?"

"...Um, because you wanted to see my face?"

"Obviously it's for business. What I mentioned before, the PMC."

"Ah, that..."

"I looked into it, but there are some legal complications. Though I think this could be resolved with some lobbying..."

"To be precise, it's not just about hiring but also taking responsibility for training them."

"Yes, I've already completed the legal registration under the name Yu-seong PMC. But Da-in, are you sure this is okay?"

"Yeah. Actually, whether it's okay or not, we absolutely have to do this if we're thinking about the future."

"...If you say so, I understand."

I had this conversation with her while sipping a cup of tea.

...In the latter part of the original story, public safety in the Republic of Korea deteriorates to the point of near collapse.

And one of the reasons for that is B and C-class villains.

You might wonder how much trouble B and C-class villains could cause, but while they might not be strong enough to lift an airplane by themselves, they're still very powerful against civilians.

And in a few years, as more people awaken abilities, countless lower-tier villains will cause so much trouble that the government and the association will reach a point where they can barely maintain public order. On top of that, lower-tier heroes don't make much money, and due to the strict public sentiment toward heroes where they're criticized for even minor mistakes, the number of heroes remains low. So ability users hide their powers and eventually turn to the path of villainy...

In other words, considering the chaos that will unfold in the future.

We need to take some kind of action before they all go crazy. And since that surplus power will become quite important later, we need to preserve it.

And this is where Lee Seol-ah's role becomes significant.

If she can use her capital and recognition to gather them, it will be very helpful later.

Actually, I've been waiting for the right timing to ask this favor, and I decided to use her previous mistake as an opportunity to request this.

Well, I'm sure Lee Seol-ah doesn't want Korea to fall into chaos either. After all, Korea will soon be hers.

This will probably be the first business I do after revealing my identity. Since Stardust already knows my name, there's nothing to hold back anymore.
