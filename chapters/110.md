At the center of the pink storm, where purple electricity-like flashes flickered, an overwhelming silence hung in the air.

"......"

Staring at me with trembling eyes was the Moon Priestess, the villain who had already devastated Seoul three times with magical storms.

Unlike the seemingly impenetrable storm that no hero could stop, the woman at its center looked fragile and young.

"...What exactly are you saying..."

She asked me with a slightly trembling voice.

Judging by how the magic circles floating around her wavered unsteadily in the air, she seemed extremely confused.

And now was the perfect moment to press forward.

Still grinning, I walked toward her step by step and spoke.

"Moon Priestess, or rather, Baek Eunwol. I know all about the Moonlight Cult. I even know about the cult leader."

As I spoke and walked toward her, she flinched and took a small step back.

I smiled and told her:

"Eunwol. Please be honest. You're suffering because of the Moonlight Cult, aren't you? You actually hate committing these acts of terror, don't you? But you have no choice because of the cult leader."

As I continued striding forward, I eventually reached right in front of her.

She had been taking small steps backward but now had nowhere else to retreat, trembling before me.

The magic circles that had been floating in the air had long since wavered and disappeared.

"...Don't you want to stop being controlled by them and live your own life?"

I said that while standing before her.

She could only tremble slightly.

Just as in the original story, she was someone who froze when confronted directly.

She had probably lived a life of constant imprisonment, being manipulated by the cult leader and forcing fake smiles for cult members.

This might even be her first direct conversation with another person.

Baek Eunwol looked up at me, still confused.

Hmm, I've been pushing hard until now, so maybe I should try a gentler approach.

Having said all that, I knelt on one knee to meet her eyes.

To her, still unable to gather her thoughts, I smiled.

This time, I spoke quietly, gently, as if coaxing her.

"I will save you."

"Leave the Moonlight Cult behind and come with me."

"I will defeat the cult leader."

"So. Won't you take my hand and start a new life together?"

I finished speaking and extended my hand.

Looking at me still smiling.

Though she was still trembling slightly, her eyes seemed calmer than before.

And then, after briefly looking at me with her red eyes,

"...First, I don't know how you know so much about me. I don't know, but still."

After saying that, she continued with a slightly trembling voice.

"...Will you really help me?"

"Yes. That's right."

"Why... why? I'm just... a monster..."

She suddenly spoke with a self-deprecating voice.

Yes. Now's my chance.

I suddenly grabbed her hand as she tried to shrink away before a stranger she'd just met.

Startled by my sudden grasp, she flinched.

But I pretended not to notice her reaction and boldly exclaimed:

"No. You are not a monster."

"Others might not know, but I do. I've seen how, even while causing all these acts of terror, you've been trying to minimize casualties."

"Who could call you a monster when you've resisted until the end, even while being controlled by the cult leader?"

"Eunwol. You are not a monster. Don't blame yourself. So..."

I grabbed her as she stood there and pulled her into my embrace.

"Don't cry."

And after hearing my words,

She was quietly crying.
