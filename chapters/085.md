Chapter 85 - Agitation And Fabrication

Whether she could stop the falling missile, whether she could do it in such a depleted state.

To be honest, she couldn't be certain.

"....."

The missile was immediately visible as soon as she took flight.

Seeing it approach right in front of where they were, she gritted her teeth.

Could she stop that?

No, that wasn't what mattered.

Once she'd made her decision, she had no choice but to stop it.

For the civilians who might get caught in it.

For the city that might be completely destroyed by it.

And... to protect a man who might die because of it.

So she headed toward the incoming missile rather than fleeing from it.

And in that split second.

She hurled herself toward it.

....

Honestly, she didn't even know how she did it.

The gut-wrenching pain she felt the moment she collided with it, the sensation of her flesh burning, the impact that reverberated through her head.

It was similar to when she had lifted an airplane before, no, even more severe.

Especially since she was already weakened from the prolonged battle.

A situation where losing consciousness wouldn't be surprising.

However, she endured with superhuman willpower.

After colliding with her, the missile had slowed down, but it was still moving forward.

To stop it.

Even in a state where she felt like she might lose consciousness, she exerted her power to its limit.

Under normal circumstances, it would have been absolutely impossible.

But because she was someone who grew stronger the more adversity she faced. Because she was someone who became stronger when she had someone to protect.

She.

With strength from an unknown source.

Finally.

Stopped the missile.

And then.

Now almost exceeding her own limits.

She sent the missile flying back into the sky.

"........"

It was unclear how much her ability had amplified in this brief moment.

The missile she launched flew high, high into the sky, becoming a star in the heavens.

And then, she collapsed.

"....Ah."

She let out a short groan as she fell.

The moment she launched the missile back, her body, having exerted its ability beyond its limits, seemed to lose all function, as if all her strength had drained away.

Having collapsed in mid-air, falling was the natural consequence.

So she closed her eyes tightly as she plummeted.

If she fell like this, would she be okay?

No matter how much stronger her body was compared to an ordinary person, she couldn't guarantee she'd be safe falling from this height. She probably wouldn't die, but... she should prepare to spend a few days in the hospital.

As she was bracing herself for the impact that would soon come.

Whoosh.

Instead of impact, she felt someone's hands gently embracing her.

".....?"

At this unexpected sensation, she slightly opened her eyes to see what was happening.

And what she saw was.

Egostic, holding her in his arms, floating in mid-air.
