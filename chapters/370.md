Chapter 370 - A World Of If

"...Damn it. There's no answer."

In the burning city.

With her singed blonde hair fluttering, Stardust muttered quietly.

[Heeheeheeheeheehee! Where are you hiding?]

"North Sea Ice Lady. What's the situation now?"

As Stardust spoke with a quiet, hoarse voice,

Lee Seol-ah's heavy sigh and voice came through the earpiece.

["...There are no more survivors in that sector. Withdraw. We'll deal with Reaper later."]

"..."

With those words,

As Lee Seol-ah delivered the facts in a heavy voice,

Stardust gritted her teeth and finally muttered as if spitting out the words.

"Understood. I'll withdraw..."

["Yes. Come back safely."]

With that, the communication ended.

...As Stardust was about to leave the collapsed city where only the villain's psychic entity howled noisily.

Suddenly, with a crackling of lightning in front of her.

KWAAANG

"...Haa."

A woman with orange hair landed, embracing a brown-haired woman beside her.

That woman glaring at her.

"...Electra."

At Stardust's words upon seeing the woman.

"I came because I heard something happened... Are you running away now? What happened here?"

Electra, the woman with the orange ponytail, asked with a frown while looking around.

And to her question.

"...There are no more survivors here. We're withdrawing."

Stardust answered calmly, in a voice that seemed resigned.

"What? Don't talk nonsense!"

To Electra's rough response,

The brown-haired woman in a white gown standing beside her grabbed her with pleading eyes and said.

"...I think it's true, Se-hee. There's no survivor response here now."

"Damn it..."

At Choi Se-hee's muttering,

Stardust, who had been standing with a cold face, tried to walk past her, but.

"...You."

...As Electra opened her mouth with an angry voice directed at her, she paused for a moment and turned her head back.

"What have you been doing? All this time?"

"..."

"What have you been doing while all these people were dying? What have you been doing?!"

"...I didn't even have time to do anything."

"Se-hee, stop it!"

The brown-haired woman grabbed Electra's hand as she began to fume.

But despite her gesture, Electra didn't stop; instead, she became even angrier.

"The world has come to this state, and even a villain nobody like me is stepping up like this. What are all those great association and heroes doing? Huh?"

"Choi Se-hee!!"

"....."

The woman beside her suddenly shouted.

"...Well, I'm just frustrated."

Electra grumbled in a somewhat smaller voice after the loud outburst.

...Stardust glanced at the woman standing next to Electra.

She already knew that brown-haired woman.

The woman called the Saintess of Villains. Lee Ha-yul.

Since Choi Se-hee, also known as Electra, escaped during the last mass breakout, the two had been acting together.

...Now, none of it mattered anymore.

"Haah..."

After scratching her head, Choi Se-hee

Finally exhaled roughly, turned her back, and walked toward the collapsed city.

"Fine. I was stupid to expect anything. Let's go, Ha-yul."

"Okay. ...Ms. Stardust, I apologize on her behalf."

With lightning flashing, Electra and the Evil Saintess disappeared.

Left alone again, Stardust stared at the collapsed city with unfocused eyes for a moment before moving on.

...She was tired.

The Republic of Korea seemed to have no hope left.

After the Moonlight Church's Gate incident, South Korea, which had been their base, fell into complete chaos. The entire country was attacked by monsters, and Korea, which already had few heroes, suffered devastating damage.

Even after that, Lee Seol-ah managed to hold on by relocating the capital several times, but the situation was unlikely to improve.

Especially after Shadow Walker, one of the few A-class heroes, disappeared, she had been struggling to hold on alone.

Even that seemed hopeless.

To make matters worse, the breakout of villains in South Korea occurred.

Dozens of them gathered to take revenge on Stardust herself, destroying cities, and even the White Witch who planned it all. Particularly, there were too many sacrifices in killing her.

She thought things would be okay now.

That now, things would really be alright.

Until the Inflation incident suddenly occurred, causing all ability users' powers to go berserk.

"....."

After that incident, not just South Korea but the entire world fell into chaos.

The emergence of Kathedal, a global villain alliance.

Therefore, it was now difficult to hope for foreign aid like during the Demon King's Castle era. Everyone was struggling.

And.

The villains of Korea, who had gained overflowing power,

Began to appear in groups, destroying all inhabited places one by one.

Too many for even the strengthened Stardust to stop.

In just three months, the entire country had been ravaged.

...To the point where moderate villains like Electra were no longer pursued or charged with crimes.

"...."

Stardust looked up at the reddened sky with haggard eyes.

Sometimes.

She has delusions that the world revolves just to torment her.

When one problem is covered up, another arises.

When she mourns one death, ten more die.

When she thinks there's nowhere left to fall, the world shows her an even deeper bottom.

As if the world desires her despair.

'.....'

Where did it all go wrong?

How did this world end up like this?

With such thoughts.

She quietly closed her eyes and rose into the sky.

Someday.

Desperately praying that a peaceful life would come to her too.
