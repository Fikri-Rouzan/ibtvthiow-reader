Yes. I'd been saving my abilities for today. ...Though it felt like they were already depleted.

I held onto my hat, floating high in the sky, looking down and muttering to myself.

A massive monster standing in the middle of the city.

And dozens of my comrades fighting it, emitting various lights—light blue, yellow, black, purple.

The truth is, I know.

I know that their attacks aren't doing much damage to that thing.

The creature has some resistance to abilities, so even with everyone's efforts, it's not enough to bring it down. There's a reason it's the final boss of this calamity.

But.

This is enough to distract it and interfere with its movements.

And this time we've bought...

I smiled quietly as I watched the yellow light cutting through the dark sky, flying toward the monster.

...Yes.

This way, Stardust can attack without any interference.

Flash.

BOOM.

The monster's screams erupted as a yellow light bright enough to illuminate the dark sky burst around it.

[KYAAAAAAAAAAH!]

Yes. This is exactly it.

The giant monster, known as the "Beast of Moonlight," is one of the god's creations.

For some reason, the Moonlight Church leader managed to control it around this time, and it has the serious problem of being resistant to all ability users. In the original story, almost all surviving S-class heroes from America and around the world couldn't defeat it despite their combined efforts.

However, Stardust was different.

Unlike other ability users, Stardust had a different origin for her power. A power of starlight fundamentally different from other supernatural abilities—specially bestowed upon her by a god.

Her attacks actually worked on that thing.

That's why I went to such lengths to help her grow as much as possible before this moment.

And now.

BOOM.

BOOM.

I found myself muttering as I watched the brilliant yellow flashes.

Yes. Stardust is doing amazingly well right now.

All her efforts haven't been in vain.

Everyone watching this battle can have hope.

Curious, I checked the chat once, but was shocked to see messages flooding in from all languages and quickly closed it. Practically the whole world is watching...

Yes. Anyway, let's do our best until the end.

To defeat the Beast of Moonlight and bring down the Moonlight Church leader to end this Gate crisis.

...Well. I'll just think of it as dying after finishing this today. Life isn't much more than that anyway.
