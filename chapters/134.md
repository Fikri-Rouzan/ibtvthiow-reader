Shin Ha-ru currently suspects that I'm Egostic.

So what's the solution?

Simple. I just need to prove that Egostic is carrying out a terrorist attack somewhere else while I'm here. That secures my alibi.

However, this attack needs to happen extremely quickly while also not leaving even a hint of suspicion with Stardust.

So the important thing is to buy time.

After quickly formulating the plan, I explained it to our Ego-stream members and had them execute it immediately.

Despite my sudden instructions, they didn't panic and moved quickly like my own limbs.

Thank you. I really did pick the right people.

Since staying too long might raise suspicion, I quickly conveyed what needed to be said and went back outside the bathroom.

Then I took a deep breath.

Right. Now I just need to wait.

After preparing myself mentally, I went outside.

There she was, lying on a sun bed, looking somewhat troubled and deep in thought.

...I hope she's not thinking about how to catch and lock me up.

I needed to divert my thoughts before they got stranger.

With that determination, I approached her and spoke first.

After some time had passed.

BOOM-

The terrorist attack had begun.

...Of course, there was a bit of an awkward situation when we parted, but what matters is that we did part ways.

With Shin Ha-ru gone as well,

I stood alone on the now-bustling beach and gathered my thoughts.

Alright. Let's stick to the plan.

Egostic had appeared? Really?

'Really?'

Yes.

This is strange. It doesn't make sense.

Though there had been some delay, still.

Since they said he had been carrying out this attack continuously before she arrived...

It was more likely that this Egostic was real, and the man she had been talking to at the beach wasn't Egostic. There couldn't be two people in the same place at the same time.

Yes. That must be the real Egostic.

....Really?

Without realizing it, she hovered in the sky, watching Egostic who was still ranting and carrying out his attack below, apparently not noticing her.

...That's supposed to be Egostic?

Intuition.

Intuitively, she felt something was off.

A strange feeling. Yes.

Surely the man down there must be Egostic.

But why.

Why doesn't he feel like Egostic?

It was just intuition again. If someone asked why she felt that way, she could only say, "It just feels that way." It was closer to a baseless assumption or delusion.

But up in the sky, feeling the cold wind.

She continued to be captivated by this strange feeling.

She couldn't believe it.

Egostic was definitely... with her at the beach.

So.

If she got closer, if she could face him directly at a close distance. If she could exchange a few words with him.

Wouldn't she know?

Wouldn't she be certain?

Whether the man in front of her was Egostic or not?

Yes.

If it was her, who had seen Egostic more than anyone else.

...Perhaps, if it was her, who had just been having a casual conversation with him.

Surely just by facing him, she would be able to tell immediately.

With that conviction.

She called out loudly to Egostic, who was still throwing what seemed to be bombs or smoke bombs while hovering at one side of the street.

"Egostic!"

Hearing her voice, he momentarily froze, stopped throwing bombs, and spun around.

As he turned to look at her, she was seized by an even stranger sensation.

....Is that really Egostic?

It's strange, but yes.

She could confirm it more accurately if she got closer to him.

With that thought, as she was flying toward him at lightning speed.

[Hyaah-!!!!]

"....!"

Sensing a shadow and an ominous presence suddenly appearing in front of her, Shin Ha-ru hastily pulled back mid-flight.

And then.

THUD-

With a tremendous sound, something with a huge body blocked her path.

[Hahaha! I'm your match, lady!]

A massive, heavy mechanical suit reminiscent of a monster.

Gleaming cleanly in silver, it had fallen from the sky.

In its center was an image of a star being shattered in half by a red line.

And in contrast to its massive and intimidating appearance, a young girl's voice came through the speaker inside.

[Stardust? It's been a while! I, South Silver, will stop you with this new, your nemesis—Star Breaker!]

The triumphant voice of what must be a young girl inside the mechanical device.

Hearing that voice, something welled up inside Stardust.

....My nemesis is not you, but Egostic.

So.

"Move."

But the mechanical suit, as if determined not to give way, stood in front of Egostic with arms spread.

Stardust clenched her fist, suppressing her anger.

...Fine. Let's deal with this quickly and get to Egostic.

With that, she charged at the so-called Star Breaker.

And it, in turn, pulled out missiles from its mechanical arms.

The battle had begun.
