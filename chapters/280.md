"....."

After skimming through the heated community reactions, I could only sigh.

...The problem with this post is that it's not even from my fan cafe. This is just what I see when I visit any random community site.

'...Well, it's not like this is super important anyway.'

I decided to just accept it.

After all, I've practically raised Stardust, stopped the Moon Cult leader, and joined Kathedal as a villain. The public opinion doesn't matter anymore. A few terrorist attacks and the opposing opinions will quickly emerge again. Probably...?

As I was thinking about this, I suddenly remembered something that had been bothering me.

[I'm sorry, sponsor. I... I think I need some time to think.]

It was that woman who had been running the anti-Egostic broadcast I was funding. She contacted me saying she had doubts and needed a break from broadcasting.

I was surprised since no one criticized me as deliciously as she did, but before I could convince her, she apologized and disappeared.

...She'll come back, right?

"Well, it doesn't really matter anyway."

I leaned back on the sofa, crossed my legs, and muttered while sipping my tea.

In reality, it's just conspiracy theory channels and some tabloid media hyping me up as amazing, but other countries aren't any different. Perhaps because I spoke to Kathedal, other world-class villain alliances also helped heroes prevent this catastrophe.

So... by that logic, everyone's a hero. You're all accomplices too. Even if they want to say I'm not a villain, they can't criticize me because they did the same thing.

Right. People tend to overestimate villains. Villains are people too. Do you know why most villains in this world cause terror? It's one of three reasons: they want attention, they're psychopathic killers, or they dream of world domination. Sometimes all three.

Anyway, all of this is only possible if the world remains intact. Which means we villains don't want the world to be destroyed by someone else.

In other words, any other villain in my position would have stopped the Moon Cult leader without hesitation. So I felt no shame whatsoever.

"But oppa."

"What?"

"Since we all worked so enthusiastically to stop it, isn't it kind of dishonest to hope people won't praise you? There were already plenty of people who liked you before."

Seo-eun, who had heard my thoughts, asked with genuine curiosity.

"...Well, that's not what's important."

I just said that while taking another sip of tea.

What matters is that we stopped that horrible disaster. Everything else after that is trivial and can be ignored... or so I'd been thinking recently.

So I took quite a long break.

...To the point where I started wondering if it was okay to rest this much. Shouldn't I be doing something as a villain?

Hmm, and I should start planning my big retirement plan. And prepare for the next terror attack.

Just as I was starting to think about these things.

An incident occurred.

[Breaking news! An unidentified villain has appeared and is threatening people! The Association has announced they will dispatch Stardust soon, and citizens are advised to stay away...]

A few months after the terrible disaster, as buildings were being reconstructed and society was finding stability again, villains who had been biding their time started to become active once more.

I was quietly resting during my terror-vacation period, but other villains wouldn't do the same. As a result, terrorist attacks started increasing again like before. ...Even more than in the original story. Back then, many villains would have been a meal for monsters, but now they were all still alive, which created problems.

So I was carefully observing these villains, but they were all just weak. Anyway, they're all one-hit wonders against our Stardust's star-fist, right?

That's why when I heard the breaking news about a terror attack on TV, I wasn't particularly moved. Just another extra villain #1, whatever.

...Yes. That's what I thought.

"Wait...."

Until I saw his outfit on the broadcast.

Wait, isn't that guy...?

Instantly grasping the situation, I immediately got up and grabbed my cape and mask.

"Huh? Where are you going?"

To Seo Ja-young's question as she was lying next to me eating snacks, I replied while changing my outfit.

"Oh, I'm just going to stop that guy real quick."

I need to step in for this one.

These gimmick-type villains can't be handled by Stardust's power alone.

Sigh. My break is over now. Right. I've rested long enough. My body's getting restless anyway, time to get back to work.

...Of course, I had an ominous feeling that if I stepped in now, the public would again create forced narratives about me being a hero and make a fuss, but Stardust was more important to me than that.

Come to think of it, this will be the first time I see Stardust this year since last time.

...After parting ways last time, meeting again like this might be a bit awkward. Haha.

"Seo-eun, oppa will be back soon."

"Huh? Where are you going, oppa?"

After saying goodbye to Seo-eun who had just arrived in the living room, I teleported away.

With my camera in hand.
