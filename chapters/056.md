"Yes. Do you happen to know... the Mango Squad? It's fans who like Mango... no, Lord Egostic. For reference, everyone here, including me, is part of the Mango Squad!"

Ah...

Please stop.

My toes are curling.

Who does this in front of the person involved...

However, I am a pro. A pro of the villain world.

Being a pro... I didn't waver and answered with a refreshing smile.

"I see. It is truly an honor that there are people who like me. However, um... I'm asking because I'm genuinely curious, do you happen to know that I am a Villain?"

I struck at her unexpected blind spot here.

No, I'm thankful they support me.

But have they actually forgotten that I'm a villain?

I thought these 'Mango Squad' guys were just concept trolls on the internet, but my opinion has just changed.

Sparkling your eyes at a terrorist while being held hostage at a terror scene cannot be called a concept.

This is just madness...

Why would someone who looks normal and popular do something like this?

At my question filled with pure curiosity, she asked back in a bewildered voice, as if startled.

"Huh? Wasn't *Oppa* the S-Rank Hero Apple Mango?"

I almost lost my mind hearing that.

No, I can't listen to this anymore.

I'm running low on cringe resistance. Please stop.

"Yes!!! I heard you loud and clear. That is a very interesting story. Would you like to say one last word?"

I hurriedly threw out the closing comment.

My goal was definitely to plant guilt in the public who were mindlessly trying to take the 100,000 won, but somehow the conversation is lightyears away from that.

It's right to end this quickly.

When I made the gesture to wrap things up, the girls behind her whined, asking why we were finishing already.

Hmm. I think this is the most painful moment since I fell into this world. I'm really going crazy.

Of course, I didn't express those feelings outwardly and just kept a bright smile.

Thank god. The mask covers half my face.

The corners of my mouth behind the mask are trembling furiously.

"Ah!"

Then she started rummaging through the bag in front of her.

Seeing that, the other girls in the back also realized something and rummaged through their bags together. What, what is it?

What they took out of their bags were...

Notebooks?
