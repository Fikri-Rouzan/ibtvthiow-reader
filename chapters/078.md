Why am I so annoyed?

Right.

Yes. The more she thought about it, the more she realized she had no reason to be angry. So why did she feel so bad?

What did that guy, Egostic, have to do with her?

And so, she began to reflect on Egostic.

Egostic.

He was a Villain who appeared shortly after she became a Hero, just as she was starting to get used to the job. That was Egostic.

He was different from other Villains who simply destroyed the city or took hostages to demand money.

At first, some even wondered if he was a dark hero because he only went around killing other Villains, but those rumors vanished after his first act of terror.

And during that first terror incident.

At that time, he had specifically pointed out Stardus herself.

No, come to think of it, it was even before then. He had been targeting her through the messages he left behind after killing other Villains.

And in the next terror incident, he had pointed her out. As if it were only natural.

Moreover, when she was about to step out with her face exposed, he somehow knew and appeared to help.

In the next terror incident, he even contacted her directly, saying he trusted her and that she was the one who completed him.

In the end, he had even thrown himself into danger to save her.

Right. Until now, every time Egostic caused an incident, he had always singled out Stardus and remained by her side.

As she reflected on that, she suddenly realized something.

Just as Egostic always seemed to pay attention only to her among all the Heroes.

She, too, had focused most on Egostic among all the Villains.

"....."

Right.

Come to think of it, her life as a Hero had reached a point where it was difficult to discuss without Egostic.

She couldn't even clearly remember what she did before meeting him; she just captured one Villain after another.

But Egostic.

Her history with him had grown quite long.

A Villain who escaped every time using Teleportation, a skill with high mobility, only to cause terror again.

Yet there were no casualties, he eliminated other Villains, and he even saved people under strange pretenses.

He was a man full of secrets, a man who always singled her out and was constantly entwined with her.

Stardus, who had unconsciously begun thinking of him again, realized something.

She could no longer imagine a life without Egostic.

Egostic causing an incident, calling for her, and then her chasing after him had become so familiar.

Before she knew it, a life without that cycle was almost unimaginable.

It wasn't him calling for "Stardus" directly.

Right. From a certain point onward.
