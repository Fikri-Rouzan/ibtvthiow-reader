After returning from Kathedal.

I was on the phone with Li Xiaopeng, who had called the number we'd exchanged, presumably after checking the file I'd sent him.

As expected, he was saying he would definitely repay this favor.

Yes, I knew he would react this way. Given his persona, he's not the type to just say thanks and move on after receiving something like this. He'll definitely try to repay it. All to maintain his image as a man of honor.

Apart from that, well, he might have judged that recognizing my potential and becoming friendly with me wouldn't be a bad thing. As I always say, at Kathedal I deliberately make myself appear mysterious and powerful. Dropping bombshell information was just an extension of that.

Anyway, as planned, Li Xiaopeng has become favorable toward me, so that's good.

...He's actually showing an even more favorable reaction than I expected, making me flinch without realizing it. Given his calculating nature, this might also be an act?

In any case, he responded positively to the hint I dropped about a three-way alliance, and we agreed to meet for drinks when we both had time, ending the call.

"Phew..."

After putting my phone back in my pocket.

I had stepped out onto the balcony for the call, and now I leaned my arms on the railing, looking out at the scenery.

Good, everything's going according to plan. The formation of an East Asian villain alliance seems within reach.

If this happens, the Republic of Korea will be a little safer than before. Having international allies is better than having none, right?

...The build-up was a bit long, but I'm glad it succeeded. Especially considering how things went to hell in the original story because of villains from Japan and China, this is necessary.

Of course, it's not like governments joining forces officially—it's just villains forming an alliance—but since these villains essentially represent the positions of their respective national leaders, isn't it basically the same thing?

Of course, there was the minor problem that I wasn't committing terrorism because I was preparing for this, but that wasn't a big issue. Saving the country comes first; that other stuff isn't as important.

"Well... I guess everything's pretty much resolved now."

Now our Ego-squad has started recruiting second-generation members, and I've taken the first step toward cooperation by meeting with the Director.

On top of that, the East Asian villain alliance that will launch by connecting Katana and Li Xiaopeng is starting to take shape.

Everything I've been preparing for a long time is falling into place one by one.

So maybe now I can do what I want to do.

For a hero, reviewing one's own battles is extremely important.

If you can review your combat process through broadcast stations or other video media, you should definitely take advantage of it.

That's how you can reflect and do image training, thinking "I should have dodged like this then" or "I should have attacked like this then," allowing you to advance to a higher level.

It was also one of the training methods recommended by the Association.

That's why Stardust was watching videos of her battles today, using her spare time in her office.

The video she was reviewing this time was from the recent incident where the Seoul Trade Center transformed into a Demon King's Castle—a significant crisis for her. The terrorism event when that Demon King guy appeared.

Since he was such a formidable opponent that Stardust almost lost, she needed to periodically review this video to reassess her skills.

[Anyway, you've worked hard, so take a rest. Does it make sense for my arch-enemy to be defeated by some other villain? I'll take care of the rest.]

"..."

...Of course, the scene she always focused on wasn't the battle itself but the final moment when Egostic flew in and stood in front of the Demon King to protect her... but that was just a misconception. A misconception.

"Ahem, ahem."

And so, with a slightly reddened face, she finished her review today.

...No matter how many times she heard what Egostic said then, it had the power to embarrass her. Really, is he a hero or what? Not that she disliked it, but...

"Sigh..."

After finishing watching the video, she turned off the monitor.

Today again, she had watched videos featuring Egostic, making various excuses to herself, and now she was sitting in her chair with her arms crossed and a pouty expression.

...So.

When on earth is he coming back?

It had been months since Egostic last showed his face.

Since leaving that Post-it note during the Scream Maker incident, there had been no sign of whether he was dead or alive.

...Egostic could easily find out what Stardust was doing just by searching the internet, so why couldn't she know what he was doing? Isn't that unfair?

As she tapped the edge of her desk, lost in thought, she sighed and muttered to herself without realizing it.

"...When are you coming back..."

And at that moment, she didn't know.

That she would be seeing Egostic very soon.
