\# Stardust's Flight Training

At the exact moment when Stardust would be flying while dripping with sweat...

I was sitting in a pre-prepared set, quietly reviewing my plan.

The purpose of my current terrorist act was simple.

To increase Stardust's flying speed. That's it.

'There's not much time left until the gate opens.'

As much as Stardust had grown stronger, I needed to develop elements beyond combat as well.

That's why I deliberately analyzed her average flying speed, then set a time limit that was a bit tighter than that. Our protagonist grows in times of crisis, after all.

'And...'

A first-class villain must prepare many things for a single terrorist act. Of course, there was something else to train as well.

Namely, evacuation training for the public.

With the gate incident approaching, it's also important for citizens to quickly escape to bunkers when that day comes. If they're not accustomed to this, evacuation will be slow, and they might get torn apart by monsters emerging from the gate while trying to flee.

That's why my condition was that the faster people evacuated from the area where I claimed a missile would fall, the more time I would give.

It was a two-birds-with-one-stone approach: making citizens practice quick evacuation while also giving myself the flexibility to extend the time for Stardust if needed. I couldn't actually drop a real missile on the city.

Anyway, that's why I deliberately chose a city where all the underground bunkers were completed... but let's set that aside for now.

'About 10 minutes left now...?'

I checked the remaining time and muttered to myself.

Currently, the screen only showed the missile floating in the air and the countdown timer, without showing my appearance.
