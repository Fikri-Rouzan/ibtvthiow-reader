After negotiating with Celeste and securing her cooperation, I returned home and was busy with various tasks.

Phase 4 was now looming on the horizon, but there was still some time left.

Before that, there were things I had been thinking about.

First, infiltrating Kathedal to gain control of the villain faction.

Second, cooperating with Lee Seol-ah to stabilize the Republic of Korea.

And third...

'Ahem...'

...it was about Stardust.

Stardust. The protagonist of this world, beautiful, with a lovely personality, and just all-around wonderful.

Phase 4 was when she would finally reach her peak after all her struggles in the original story.

Both in terms of her suffering and her recognition.

'How exactly did that go again...'

Right.

Gods.

The Sun God, Moon God, and Star God.

These three gods don't exist in this world. Like when I saw the Moon God before, they exist in another dimension.

And currently, the Sun God was trying to cross over from another dimension into this world.

To destroy this world.

In other words, the Sun God's body was gradually getting closer to our world.

And Stardust's ability—the power to grow stronger in the presence of those with the power of the sun—more precisely, it was an ability that grew proportionally stronger depending on how 'close' someone with the power of the sun was.

So now that the Sun God's body, made of pure solar power, was getting closer to our world?

Literally.

It means Stardust's power would suddenly go berserk.

'In the original story...'

Stardust experienced explosive growth, suddenly reaching first place in the unofficial world hero rankings. Of course, she's already among the top 10 in skill, so it makes sense.

In other words, Phase 4 is completely Stardust's stage.

An epic battle between Kathedal, who wants to destroy the world, and Stardust standing against them.

Of course, I had no intention of letting Stardust struggle like in the original story. Why else would I have joined Kathedal?

Of course, I had my reasons. I had worked hard my entire life in this world to be perceived as a bad guy by Stardust. For things to turn out like this. A complete miscalculation in my plan. Of course I wanted to deny it.

As such, I should have rejected her confession on the spot. Both to perfect my plan... and because I had Ego-stream.

However...

"....."

That look in her eyes.

The look she gave me that day contained desperation.

A desperation that made me instinctively feel that if I rejected her confession, something worse would happen.

...And, to be honest.

"Ahem..."

I also liked Stardust... probably much more than she thought. I just gritted my teeth and suppressed my personal feelings because it was work. After all, my goal in this life was her happiness. It was also my motivation for being in this world...

That day.

'Are you okay...?'

That meeting, not long after I fell into this world.

I had already decided then.

That I would use this life for her, who shone so brilliantly before me.

In terms of dating, it would be the ultimate secret relationship.

At night.

Stardust, Shin Ha-ru, lying in bed looking at her phone, unconsciously smiled with a snort.

The Egostic fan cafe she used to visit to find information about Egostic.

...And even now, after learning where Egostic lived. She was still habitually checking the fan cafe.

Of course, the difference from before was...

Now, she could look down at those wondering about Egostic's whereabouts much more comfortably, with a smile on her face.

As she smiled while scrolling through the posts, she suddenly had a thought, got up from her seat, and sighed.

"...Haah."

...Come to think of it, what was the point?

She hadn't seen Egostic—Da-in—since their last meeting either.

Stardust played with her blonde hair, thinking with slightly vacant eyes.

She wanted to be with him. She wanted to know what Da-in liked, what he disliked, what he did in his daily life. She wanted to know everything and do everything together.

However...

As things stood now, even proper communication was difficult. Because of that damn Ego-stream.

"....."

...Should I just blow it all up? Announce that Egostic and I are dating?

As she thought this with cold eyes, Stardust suddenly came to her senses, shook her head vigorously, and thought with a blushing face.

...That would still be problematic. It would be troublesome if citizens lost trust in her. A hero dating a villain. All kinds of criticism would pour in from the public...

Still...

'...Still.'

Even if they couldn't meet, she at least wanted to contact him...

Late at night.

As she rolled around in bed wearing her pajamas, holding her smartphone and continuing these thoughts.

By the time it had become dawn.

With a slightly hazy mind from being half-asleep, she suddenly had a thought.

"...Wait."

Come to think of it, it's dawn right now.

Da-in would be alone at dawn too.

...So, wouldn't it be okay to contact him?

After going through this thought process, she entered the messaging app with a slightly pounding heart.

His phone number that she had received during their last meeting.

'...Come to think of it, he might be sleeping right now, right?'

That the villain's nightmare who wore such a cold expression at terror sites, instantly crushing villains—always dignified and cold-faced, receiving admiration from hero followers.

Will he reply..? What if he's already asleep?
