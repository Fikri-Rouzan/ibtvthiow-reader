\***

Han-Eun Group.

To understand Seo-eun's bad blood with this group, we first need to know what these guys were doing in the original work.

Han-Eun Group is a major corporation in the Republic of Korea, which at first glance appears to be an utterly ordinary company...

But in reality, they were secretly plotting to conquer Korea from behind the scenes—a typical "corrupt corporation" in hero stories. In other words, they were essentially a villain group.

They researched various technologies to conquer Korea.

From the monster "Behemoth" to the "Post-Human Project" for artificially developing superpowers, and the aerial weapon "Octopus."

Thus, they appeared as the final boss of Phase 1 in the comic [Stardust!], and successfully destroyed Seoul by attacking Korea with Octopus, turning it into a sea of flames.

So what's the connection between this villain group and Seo-eun?

'Seo-eun was... a victim of their "Post-Human Project."'

The Post-Human Project of the Han-Eun Group aimed to artificially create superpowers in humans.

Seo-eun has a past where she grew up as a test subject, enduring brutal biological experiments and what amounted to torture from childhood.

Somehow she finally escaped, but her mind was already wounded and she developed a distrust of humans.

Living quietly alone while festering inside, she eventually became enraged at the world that had abandoned her.

Finally, utilizing her genius intelligence that resulted from the experiments, she appeared as the S-class villain "White Witch" and caused a mass breakout from the villain prison...

'Of course, such a future won't happen now.'

Living with me, attending school, making friends, and living happily—Seo-eun wouldn't cause such incidents.

However... the Han-Eun Group was the problem.

[[Breaking News] Executives of international corporate giant Han-Eun Group "flee in the night." Evidence of national subversion found in headquarters basement... Shocking]

'What? These bastards escaped?'

Right after the power loss occurred.

Before I could do anything, the Han-Eun Group immediately fled. That is... they literally disappeared from Korea.

According to my original plan, Behemoth would have escaped from the Han-Eun Group's basement soon after, and I planned to use that opportunity to capture the Han-Eun Group... but that plan completely fell apart.

So the Association, after hearing all the circumstances from me, began tracking them, but they still haven't been caught.

Especially problematic was the escape of "Kim Sun-woo," the technology director of the Han-Eun Group and essentially the architect of the national subversion plan.

Therefore, to both clean up this threat to Korea and help Seo-eun get her revenge... I've been continuously tracking the Han-Eun Group.

And finally.
