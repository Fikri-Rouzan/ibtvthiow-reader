...Perhaps because I'd coughed up blood earlier, my throat felt strained, so I spoke in a slightly lower voice.

"Sigh... never mind."

Saying that, I sat down and picked up some documents.

With less than a week until the God's Annihilation Declaration, I needed to get work done.

What's going on?

"Seriously, why did you do that?"

In her office.

Sitting before her, he spoke as if bewildered, and Celeste turned her head away and snorted.

"Hmph. What? Can't I visit your country out of curiosity?"

...Well, it would be too embarrassing to admit she went because she was concerned about Stardust...

After she responded that way.

"No... that's not what I meant."

Speaking in a slightly irritated voice, he then.

Somehow, as if disappointed in her, he spoke in a cold voice.

"Sigh... never mind."

Thud.

With those words, he sat down without even looking at her.

...As if he had lost all affection for her.

And faced with his unexpectedly cold response, something she'd never seen before.

-Thump.

Celeste suddenly felt afraid.

...Could it be that Egostic was disappointed in her? For acting on her own?

Her eyes trembled without her realizing it.

Then she turned her head toward him again and cautiously asked.

"...Ahem. Hmm. Egostic, are you angry...?"

In response to her question, he replied coldly without even looking at her.

"What? No. If my boss wants to act on her own, what can a subordinate like me say about it?"

...In truth, Egostic genuinely thought this way, and he didn't look up simply because he was busy dealing with work.

But to Celeste, already gripped by insecurity, it sounded different.

As if he was mocking her, saying their relationship was nothing more than that of a boss and subordinate...

Of course, all of this was a huge misunderstanding on her part, caused by Egostic happening to speak a bit late at first.

...But to her, who had already convinced herself she'd angered him, this was a serious matter.

She had never once imagined their relationship could deteriorate.

'D-did Egostic... become disappointed in me?'

As she thought with an anxious expression, she seemed to hear his voice.

Hmph. Celeste. I don't need someone like you who only does whatever they want. I should just be Stardust's colleague instead. I'm disappointed, don't pretend to know me anymore. Goodbye.

'N-no!'

...Perhaps because this was her first such experience since being abandoned as a very young child.

Unknowingly triggering her trauma, Celeste suddenly spiraled and began trembling in fear.

Was it because she had recently been constantly by Egostic's side?

Or was it because Egostic was the first person to come so close to her in her life?

...Or could it be that she had different feelings for him?

Already unconsciously dependent on him.

Experiencing his coldness for the first time made her imagine a life without him again.

And that's when she finally realized.

Though she had been at the top.

She had always been lonely, unable to trust anyone.

In her solitary life pursuing only an invisible god, Egostic's presence—someone who shared her thoughts—had become such a great support.

...And now, she didn't want to return to that lonely life without him.

So.

'I-I need to apologize first...'

Finally consumed by worry, she began apologizing to Egostic in a trembling voice.

"I-I'm sorry, Egostic..."

"What?"

"...Um, hahaha. You're angry, right? I won't do it again, okay...?"

...And so, her apology as she watched his reaction.

Was probably the first apology Celeste had ever made.
