Leaving behind the still chaotic chat, I turned off the camera and prepared to go home. They'll be fired up for a few days and then forget about it anyway... Whew, I did a big job today. I'm exhausted after exerting myself for the first time in a while.

...But that's not the issue right now.

I headed toward Stardust who had collapsed. When I saw her earlier, she seemed fine except for being tired, but I should check just in case.

What I saw was her still lying in the same position, leaning against a wall in the ruins. Her eyes were closed, suggesting she had lost consciousness. Wait, surely not?

Just to be sure, I quickly ran over, knelt on one knee, and checked if she was breathing. Phew, she's breathing fine. Of course, that's to be expected, but you never know. If something had gone wrong, I would have had to go down to the underworld to save her.

With that thought, I looked down at her as she lay there breathing softly. ...It's truly amazing how someone can still look so beautiful even when covered in dirt from fighting in ruins. Is this what they call the protagonist's buff or whatever?

Anyway, she must have been exhausted to the point of fainting. In the latter part of the original story, even when she used her powers extensively, she would only be unable to move her body but never lost consciousness. Today must have been really tough.

...Well, she really did work hard today. From the morning, she fought demons underground with just an energy bar, and then in the evening, she fought the Demon King. Thinking about it again, it's hard to believe she accomplished all this in just one day. Things have really changed from the original story.

"...Good job, Ms. Stardust. As expected... it's you. Haha."

Rising from my spot, I whispered words she couldn't hear. Alright, time to go. The association staff will be here soon. I can also hear helicopter sounds—the broadcasting stations seem to be approaching now that the situation is over.

As I was about to leave in the cool night breeze, I suddenly felt concerned about Stardust lying alone in the ruins. ...It's chilly, and she might catch a cold like this? The association will arrive soon, but until then... I can't help but worry.

After a moment's hesitation, I took off the cape from my back and covered her with it. Well, I have plenty of capes at home.

Of course, I wonder what she'll think when she wakes up and sees this... But with her villain-hating mindset, she'll probably think I'm teabagging her, so it shouldn't be a problem. She'll likely get angry thinking, "Are you mocking me for lying here helplessly!"

With that thought, I couldn't help but smile before turning away from her and taking steps to teleport. Ah, I'll need another IV drip when I get home.

With that final thought, I teleported away.

And because I left immediately, I didn't know.

"...."

After I left, Stardust moved slightly.

-Rustle.

That she firmly grasped the cape placed over her with one hand.
