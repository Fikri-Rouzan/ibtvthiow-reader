After Egostic started his broadcast announcing the terror attack, the entire internet was already reporting on it.

While terror attacks by supernatural ability users are typically horrific events that face broadcast restrictions and people generally avoid watching them... Egostic's attacks were the exception.

Regardless of how his attacks unfolded, they never resulted in casualties, making them enjoyable for everyone from children to the elderly. Additionally, Egostic himself already had countless fans who found him entertaining whenever he spoke, and the peculiar relationship between him and Stardus was another noteworthy point of interest.

Furthermore, this time there was even greater attention due to rumors about his health issues following his imprisonment and escape, combined with increased international interest after the Moonlight Church attack.

[Today, I'll be raiding an amusement park!]

And so, his terror attack today was unfolding in Egostic's signature classic style, something people hadn't seen in quite a while.

After appearing in the sky above the city center, he suddenly disappeared and teleported to the sky above an amusement park below. On screen, behind his usual mask, he could be seen smiling his familiar smile while stretching his arms out above the outdoor amusement park.

[Everyone, do you know what I feel whenever I visit an amusement park? It's that there are so many thrill-seekers in our country! Wow, look over there.]

As he stretched out his arm, the camera turned as well.

There, a massive gyro drop ride was visible, rising up to the sky where he was positioned.

The circular gyro drop was slowly rising toward his position. And on it, numerous people with their legs dangling as they ascended.

After showing this scene, he turned the camera back to himself, grinned, and spoke.

[Now, everyone. Look at those people coming up. They're all voluntarily coming up to this height for the thrill. Don't you think such people deserve an even bigger thrill?]

Just as he said this, the gyro drop reached its peak.

And simultaneously.

[Ziiiiiiing]

Behind him, a massive circular magic circle appeared, matching the size of the gyro drop's circular platform.

As experts covering the terror attack were excitedly analyzing that this was the Moon Priestess's ability and that she must be collaborating with him, the magic circle began to glow.

[Kyaaaaaaah!]

The gyro drop that had reached its peak was forcibly separated from its pillar.

Then it shot upward beyond the peak and past the limit set by the amusement park, soaring high into the sky. The passengers, who were already nervous about the height of the gyro drop, began screaming with joy as it shook and rose even higher, squinting at the ground below.

And as the donut-shaped gyro drop began moving horizontally beyond the Z-axis.

Egostic, who had suddenly teleported to the center, spread his arms wide with a maniacal smile and shouted toward the camera.

[Yes, everyone, let me introduce you! Sponsored by Ego-stream, I present the first attraction of the Death Amusement Park: Space Drop!]

With his words, the gyro drop began spinning like a top while ascending into the sky.

After watching Stardus catch the last Viking ship I had thrown, I prepared to end the broadcast. This should be enough for today.

"Well everyone, that's all for today's terror attack. Take care!"

"Haa, haa... Egostic!"

In the sky as the sun was setting.

A voice calling me, breathing heavily.

As she flew toward me and called out to me, I glanced back slightly.

With the red sky as a backdrop, she had long blonde hair flowing down, and despite panting heavily, she was looking at me with blue eyes.

Before she could say anything to me.

I spoke first, still with my back turned.

"...Well then, take care, Ms. Stardus."

And.

"This is driving me crazy..."
