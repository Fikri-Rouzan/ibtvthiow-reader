Death Knight.

The pitch-black helmet on his head. The black armor covering his body, and the tattered cape behind it.

And the massive greatsword in his hand, resting on the ground, as tall as a person.

In fact, compared to the last time she fought Death Knight, Stardust herself had become much stronger too. Her abilities had evolved considerably after facing the Moon Priestess and the moon monster.

Which meant Death Knight was fighting evenly with her despite her growth.

"......"

Watching Death Knight swing his greatsword while emitting electricity, Stardust pondered.

...The constant electrical discharge during his attacks was problematic for defense, but the bigger issue was offense.

Death Knight's entire body—or should she say soul?—was flowing with current, making it quite awkward to attack. It was like hitting a hedgehog covered in thorns; not easy to deal with. Even attacking would cause damage to her.

[Come on!!! Stop running away like a coward and face me!!!]

Lightning struck at her with each swing of his sword.

Dodging them, Stardust racked her brain.

...Her stamina was already lower than usual after defeating that StarBreaker machine.

And with Death Knight appearing stronger than before, she was in quite a difficult situation.

Death Knight's already powerful attacks had become stronger with electricity flowing through them, and he could even launch ranged attacks by summoning lightning with his sword, making it hard for her to create distance and recover.

Plus, as mentioned earlier, the current flowing through his body made it quite uncomfortable for her to attack him.

In other words, the battle would inevitably be prolonged. Even if she wouldn't lose, it would take considerable time to defeat him.

And that was the worst possible scenario for Stardust, who wanted to confront Egostic as soon as possible to confirm if he was the same person she had met at the beach earlier.

"....Haah."

Turning her head to look, she could see Egostic floating far off to one side, filming the scene with a camera. He was too far away to see clearly.

In conclusion, the current situation was developing very unfavorably for her.

[Kuhahaha! Even you, Stardust, are nothing before this evolved power!!!]

Death Knight continued swinging his sword, bringing down lightning.

Yes, that lightning. Electricity was the problem. The biggest difference between the previous Death Knight and the current one.

If only he didn't have that electrical ability like before, she could have defeated him already...

As she was thinking this, something suddenly occurred to her.

...If the electricity surrounding that armor and greatsword is the problem, why not just eliminate it?

With this sudden idea, she flew around carefully observing Death Knight.

...Yes. On his back, something made of electricity was being shot from far away. That must be what's been supplying current to Death Knight all this time.

So if she dealt with whoever was supplying that current, wouldn't this all be over?

Having made this judgment, Stardust shifted her focus from Death Knight to elsewhere.

Specifically, to the source of the current that was faintly connected to Death Knight's back like a charger cable.

"....There it is."

After muttering briefly, she pushed off from midair and dove straight down.

Specifically, toward the source of the current being supplied to Death Knight.

[Hey!!! Where are you going!!!]

Ignoring Death Knight who was swinging his sword and jumping toward her from below, she plunged downward at an even faster speed, heading toward—

"Wait, what?!"

Electra, who had been sweating while supplying current to Death Knight from beside a building off to one side.

Seeing Stardust rapidly descending toward her, she clearly showed a panicked expression. Quickly spreading some kind of electromagnetic field around her body, she shouted at Stardust.

"Hey, why are you suddenly coming after me?!"

Shouting as if wronged, she hastily cut off the current supply to Death Knight and fought back against Stardust by shooting electricity in all directions.

But that wasn't much of a problem for Stardust, who had already succeeded in getting within attack range.

"...Eck."

In the end, the distracted Electra was easily knocked out by Stardust.

[No!!! Have you ever seen such a coward! Fight fair and square!!!]

Death Knight, who had rushed over too late and was swinging his sword, was no longer a major threat now that he had lost his electrical ability.

[Argh!!!]

And so, squeezing out her last bit of strength, Stardust sent Death Knight away as well.

Thus, she defeated them all and stood alone.

"Phew...."

She felt like she might collapse.

That was her impression.

It was her first time facing three villains in succession like this.

Her stamina was draining faster than expected, to the point where it was hard to move her body.

In other words, if another villain were to appear now, it would be quite difficult for her to handle.

"......"

With that thought in mind, Stardust turned her head to look around. Having already experienced being caught off guard once, she was now naturally cautious.

Fortunately, it didn't seem like any other villains were going to appear.

Which meant...

After judging that the situation was more or less resolved, Stardust looked up.

And there...

Up in the sky, she could see Egostic looking down at her and applauding.

...Finally.

Stardust, Shin Ha-ru, flew up to the sky with a slightly stiff expression.

Yes, now I finally get to see him.

Ha-ru quickly organized the situation in her mind.

She had definitely been with a man presumed to be Egostic at the beach.

And at that time, Egostic appeared in this city far away from that location. While she was with that man.

In other words, since one person cannot be in two places at once, one of them is not Egostic.

And personally, she suspected that the one in front of her was a fake created by some method, not the real Egostic.

Because she was almost certain instinctively that Da-in, whom she had seen at the beach, was Egostic.

If she could prove that the Egostic here was fake, it would confirm that Da-in was Egostic.

And whether the person floating in the air was really Egostic could be determined simply by facing him and having a conversation.

After all, she was an Egostic expert.

With that determination, Ha-ru flew up to the sky where Egostic was, and what she saw was...

"It's been a while, Ms. Stardust! You've become much stronger than before. Impressive!"

It was Egostic, smiling naturally and applauding her.

'...Huh?'

And seeing this Egostic, Ha-ru's mind went blank for a moment.

....He really seems to be the real Egostic?
