"Damn it! Damn it!!!"

In an underground base somewhere without light,

The headquarters of Shinetia, an organization pursuing knowledge.

The place was currently in chaos.

"Damn it! You said it was accurate information! That it was Kim Cheol-woo! It's not him! What are we going to do now?!"

"B-Boss! What should we do? Egostic has declared war on us! Y-You know how terrifying he is!"

"No! We're all going to die at this rate!"

After confidently broadcasting that Egostic's identity was Kim Cheol-woo,

Egostic himself immediately appeared to refute it, and thanks to his warning, the organization Shinetia was on the verge of complete destruction.

"Damn it... How did this happen?"

The leader of the organization, the boss of Shinetia,

Was currently holding his head in agony at the table.

"Didn't that woman say it was reliable information? Contact her quickly!"

"B-Boss! She's not answering. It seems she's cut ties and fled!"

Hearing his trembling subordinate's words, he clutched his head.

'...She always gave 100% accurate information so I trusted her, damn it. Of all things, she had to spread this kind of false information!'

Though he now regretted trusting her words enough to pound the ground, it was too late for regrets to change anything.

And honestly, he too had been convinced through cross-verification that Kim Cheol-woo was Egostic.

"Damn it! Are you saying it's all coincidence that Kim Cheol-woo disappeared home during Egostic's terror times and was often nowhere to be seen?"

...Had he acted too hastily in trying to bring the underground information organization Shinetia into the light? Should he have investigated more thoroughly?

It was too late for regrets now.

"Boss! What do we do now?"

As his subordinates asked anxiously,

After contemplating, he finally let go of his head and spoke while trying to maintain his composure.

"...Don't panic too much. Egostic doesn't know our location anyway. It would take him ages to find out. So..."

"B-B-Boss!! Something terrible is happening! The floor, the floor!"

"What is... oh shit."

Just as he was trying to control the people at the scene.

Ziiing-

In real time.

Suddenly, an ominous glowing purple magic circle was being drawn on one side of the underground floor.

Seeing the faintly glowing magic circle being completed in the dark basement, he instinctively realized.

That he was fucked.

"...Everyone, FIRE!!!!!!"

And so.

With his scream-like shout, everyone nearby pulled out their guns and fired at the floor where the magic circle was.

Dudududududududu-

Countless bullets raining down on the floor.

But regardless, the magic circle was already complete.

The moment the circle's line was finally connected.

Pung.

Above the magic circle, pink smoke formed.

And then, suddenly three silhouettes appeared.

"Se-hee unnie, Ja-young unnie. We've arrived."

A girl with black hair wearing priestess clothes, kneeling on the floor where the magic circle was.

Behind her stood two figures.

"So... this is the place that spread fake news about our Ego?"

A woman with orange hair, sparks flying from her body, looking ahead with a fierce gaze while cracking her knuckles.

"Yawn... Yeah. Let's get this over with quickly."

A purple-haired woman who looked sleepy, yawning with her hands in her hood.

Seeing the two who had suddenly teleported into the basement, the man spoke frantically.

"Shoot! Shoot!!!"

"Aaaaaaah!"

Ting. Ting ting ting ting.

So a shower of countless bullets rained down on where they were.

But they simply covered themselves with a pink barrier, easily blocking everything.

"Well... shall we start now?"

"Yeah. Hey, want to bet on who takes down more?"

"Sure. I'll win anyway."

Choi Se-hee and Seo Ja-young, known to the public as Electra and Mist of the Ego-stream, chatted and joked around before slowly starting to walk toward where the bullets were coming from.

One with numerous yellow lightning sparks flying from her hands in the darkness.

The other with quiet purple flames burning in the air.

"Aaaaaaah!!!"

Thanks to the Moon Priestess's barrier, the bullets bounced off as they slowly approached through the darkness.

Seeing this terrifying sight, some who had been firing their guns finally turned their backs and fled.

"Where do you think you're going?"

With crimson eyes flashing in the dark basement, Electra's body disappeared like lightning in an instant.

Kwagwagwagwang-

Lightning struck underground, and purple flames blazed.

And so, the collapse of Shinetia began.
