After I told my Ego-stream colleagues about my secret and what was going to happen in the future.

"Oppa, what exactly is this other world? Do I exist there too?"

"But what exactly is the Star God?"

"Hmm, interesting. So our dragon clan was also a creation of the Sun God."

I broke into a cold sweat for a while as I was bombarded with questions from our Ego-stream members.

Naturally, they were most interested in the fact that I came from another world, but when I told them there was almost no difference except for the existence of supernatural abilities, they seemed to lose interest. Or maybe it was because I told them they didn't exist in that world.

Anyway, when things had finally calmed down.

I called aside one of the people who had been quietly listening to me from the back and took her to my room, which was a bit away from the others.

The person I brought with me was none other than.

"Eun-wol."

It was Eun-wol.

"Yes, oppa?"

With her black hair flowing down and dressed in a white priestess outfit.

She looked up at me with a puzzled expression.

The reason I called Eun-wol aside was simple.

...I had something to tell only her.

Like Stardust and Celeste who were incarnations of gods, Eun-wol was the incarnation of the Moon God.

So I got straight to the point as soon as I saw Eun-wol.

"...Eun-wol."

"Yes, Da-in oppa. Please speak comfortably."

"Alright. ...Eun-wol. Have you received any communication from the Moon God?"

Her face suddenly turned serious at my question.

Yes.

The difference between Eun-wol and the others.

She was an incarnation of a god.

...Unlike Stardust's case, she knew she was a god's incarnation. The Moonlight Church must have told her.

To my somewhat tense question.

Eun-wol shook her head with an apologetic expression and said.

"No, oppa... The deity hasn't said anything to me specifically."

"...I see."

I quietly nodded at her words.

The Moon God.

Only indirectly mentioned in the original work, never actually appearing.

One who maintained neutrality alone and never showed their face even until the end of the original story...

'But still. Now that Eun-wol is alive.'

...I had some hope, but it seemed that was out of the question.

Well, if you think about it, the moment the Moon God intervenes, things would greatly differ from the original story... which might not be good for me since my future knowledge is my greatest weapon. So maybe it's actually fortunate.

Anyway, that's not why I called Eun-wol this time.

"Eun-wol."

As I said that, I searched my pocket and took out something I had prepared in advance.

A small object that looked like a stone, glowing silver in my hand.

Moonlight Stone.

"Do you know what this is?"

"...Yes. It's a Moonlight Stone."

Eun-wol nodded as if she obviously knew the stone I was holding.

Moonlight Stone.

A mineral used by the Moonlight Church to harness the power of the moon.

They used it to create demon kings and summon lunar monsters.

The true identity of this stone that clearly seems deeply related to the power of the moon.

'Should I call it the Philosopher's Stone?'

An essentially omnipotent substance created by the Moon God to research their magic. A substance that can store any power and never breaks.

And this.

...According to my plan, it could be quite useful.

Therefore.

I handed the Moonlight Stone to Eun-wol and asked her this.

"...Eun-wol. With your power, could you make more of these Moonlight Stones?"

Yes.

What I asked Eun-wol for was mass production of Moonlight Stones.

I was requesting if she could create these substances with the power of moon magic she possessed.

...Honestly, I was half-doubtful, but.

"Yes, Da-in oppa. I think I can do it."

Eun-wol surprisingly said she could do it with ease.

"Really?"

"Yes. If I just cast some magic... draw some magic circles..."

As she held the Moonlight Stone I had given her in her hand, looking down at it and muttering like that.

"Thank you so much, Eun-wol. It must have been an unreasonable request, I'm sorry."

Before I knew it, I grabbed her hand and said that.

...Oh wait. If I do this, Eun-wol will get embarrassed again.

As I was thinking that and about to pull my hand away.

Squeeze.

Instead, Eun-wol held my hand back as if it was nothing.

Looking up at me with her red eyes, she opened her mouth with a faintly visible smile.

"...Don't say that, oppa. My life was saved by you. So please call me whenever you need me."

Looking at Eun-wol who was saying that with a small voice, filled with sincerity while looking into my eyes.

"Thank you, Eun-wol..."

...I could only answer like that.

Inwardly, I made a promise to treat Eun-wol better from now on.

And so, I also found out how to make 'stones that can hold power.'

If Eun-wol couldn't do it, I would have had to use another method, but fortunately I don't have to.

With that thought, I moved on to the next step.

Good. I've already warned our Ego-stream members about the divine judgment.

Now, it's time to inform those in higher positions.

"Hey, Seol-ah. Do you have time?"

And so.

Next, I headed to Lee Seol-ah.

"Well. Life is a comedy when it happens to others, but a tragedy when it happens to you."

Speaking in a voice that suggested she had a headache.

"Da-in... Please tell me you have a plan... If this continues, I'll die from overwork and stress..."

With an anxious expression as if just imagining it made her want to drink, looking towards the wine cabinet, showing signs of returning to her previously cured alcoholism.

"Don't worry. Of course I've thought of a way."

"But first, I need to meet the Director."

To my naturally spoken words.

"...The Director?"

"Yes."

I answered confidently and asked her.

"You said you had a position ready for me, right? Something like a hero management manager under Yu-seong Corporation. Connect me through that."
