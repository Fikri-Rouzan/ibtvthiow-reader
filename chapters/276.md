Dark space.

"Haah..."

In that strange cosmic-like space where not even an inch ahead was visible, Stardust was being pushed somewhere again.

Before arriving at her next destination, she was thinking once more about what she had just seen.

About another dimension, a world that had perished.

"..."

...A world that was identical to the one she lived in, but somehow everything had gone wrong.

A world that had completely failed to prevent the numerous disasters that her world had successfully averted.

And the reason why that world had thoroughly collapsed, unlike her own world.

The most fundamental, the only difference between that world and hers was.

'...Egostic wasn't there.'

She murmured this thought as she drifted alone with closed eyes in the dark space.

The difference of just one person.

The single difference that Egostic didn't exist in that world had changed the fate of both worlds.

...The villains Egostic had killed at his own discretion would have become S-class villains who would later massacre thousands of people.

The disasters he had intervened to prevent would have occurred if he hadn't been there, nearly bringing Korea to the brink of extinction.

And so Stardust, having seen a world where Egostic didn't exist, finally realized.

How important Egostic was to her, once again. Belatedly.

'....Egostic.'

Yes.

Come to think of it, it was always Egostic alone who came to her in seemingly hopeless crisis situations.

Lost in these thoughts, she unconsciously recalled all the times she had spent with Egostic.

When she was despairing, thinking she couldn't stop the falling airplane, he contacted her and gave her courage with his certainty.

In the underground laboratory of Han-Eun Group, he risked his life taking attacks meant for her.

When the Han-Eun Group brought out their massive weapon, he hijacked it and stopped them.

When the Moon Priestess created a storm to attack the city, he appeared at the last moment to stop her and take her away.

When the being called Demon King tried to kill her, he suddenly appeared to block and defeat him.

In all those moments, Egostic had been with her.

He had always rushed to her aid in moments of crisis.

He even recruited or hunted other villains on her behalf.

And in this Moon Cult incident too, he ultimately helped her and led people to safety.

Yes. That's how he always was.

*Thump.*

As she continued to ponder and reflect on these thoughts.

Finally, she organized her thoughts.

...Without Egostic, millions, tens of millions would have died.

Thanks to Egostic, world peace was maintained, and people remained safe.

So then.

'Egostic... can't really be called a villain, can he?'

She found herself thinking this without realizing it.

He commits acts of terror but never harms civilians.

He deals with other villains and saves countless people.

...He always steps up for her when she's in danger.

Isn't he closer to a hero than a villain?

She drifted through this strange cosmic space with these thoughts.

...So, where am I going now? It's good that I've thought about Egostic, but I don't even know if I can return to where he is.

...Surely I won't have to drift in this strange space forever without being able to return.

As Stardust imagined such ominous thoughts.

At that moment, she unconsciously recalled his familiar face again.

...Yes. The reason she had remained intact unlike her counterpart from another world. That was because.

Always in moments like this, Egostic would come to her.

...I've relied on Egostic so much until now.

She smiled self-deprecatingly at this realization.

Why hadn't she realized it until now? Why had she always tried to think negatively about him?

In the end, the face that came to her mind in her final moment was Egostic's.

...But I can't help it. Whenever I've had these thoughts, whenever I've harbored futile hopes. As if to prove they weren't futile. As if insisting I should just trust him - you've always appeared before me with a bright smile. Making it impossible for me to dislike you even if I wanted to. You've always appeared. When I'm by your side, I've always felt reassured without even realizing it.

...I miss you.

And just as she was thinking this.

-Flash

Suddenly, a white light burst forth before her eyes.

Startled by the sudden situation, she opened her eyes.

And what appeared before her eyes was.

"...Haha, Ms. Stardust. What are you doing here?"

Wearing a black hat and a white mask covering half his face. Draped in a black cape and grinning.

The one she had been unconsciously thinking about.

...And, the one she had missed.

"I've come to save you, Ms. Stardust."

Her villain.

Her... arch-enemy.

Floating above the black space, extending his hand toward her.

It was Egostic.

And seeing him like this.

Stardust thought with dazed eyes.

...You came for me again.

As always, it was him who extended his hand to her in moments of crisis. Only him.

Looking at his smiling face.

She found herself thinking.

...How could I.

How could I possibly dislike you.

How could I.

Possibly not like you.

Whether he knew her thoughts or not.

Egostic continued smiling, his hand extended as he said to her.

"Ms. Stardust, let's go back now."

"To our home."

And looking at him.

She found herself smiling.

Smiling, she extended her hand to him.

"Yes..."

And so.

Their hands finally grasped each other.

Before she could even feel his warmth.

Flash

A white light burst forth around them, and they disappeared from that space.

It was time to return together now.

To the home they had come from.

To their Earth.
