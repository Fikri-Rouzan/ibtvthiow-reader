Stardust simply answered like she hadn't expected much anyway. Hmm, is this learned helplessness? ...Maybe I should try a different pattern next time.

While I was lost in these random thoughts, Stardust continued to look at me with that complicated expression.

'...'

'...Wait, what's wrong with her?'

But the current problem was that Stardust wasn't going to help Shadow Walker but was standing in front of me.

So while I wasn't showing it, I was slightly flustered. Come on, you should be fighting, not standing in front of me.

Just as I was thinking this, Stardust finally opened her mouth.

"...Why did you do it?"

"Pardon? Well... because I'm a villain?"

Why is she asking such an obvious question?

I answered with a smile, though slightly puzzled, and Stardust shook her head slightly as if that wasn't what she meant.

"No, I mean... that Lattice thing. Why did you stop their terror attack? Why did you do something like that?"

"Uh..."

I was caught off guard by the unexpected question.

Really? She's asking about that now? Right next to us, there's fighting and loud noises and chaos, and she's not joining in?

The content of her question was also surprising. That happened a couple of months ago. Given Stardust's personality as I know it, she should normally think, 'Whatever villains do is none of my business. It's either a new deception or not true,' and not care at all.

With those thoughts, I glanced at her again.

And met her determined gaze fixed on me.

...What's this? Why does she look so serious?

Come to think of it, I already explained during the broadcast that I knew nothing about it. Didn't she see that?

But well, that was said in front of the public to avoid potential issues.

Since we're on camera anyway, I can tell Stardust something different. Looking at her now, she doesn't seem like she'd accept such excuses anyway.

With that calculation, I quickly thought of what to say.

But if those small fries take all the public attention, my position becomes a bit difficult. So, yes, I did exert some influence. To maintain Ego-stream's exclusive status. It wasn't for the sake of Korea, obviously."

Right next to us, Mist, who's sort of my subordinate...?, and her colleague Shadow Walker are fighting as if their lives depend on it. What are we doing? While they're in pain from fighting, I'm in pain from talking too much.
