["Meanwhile, the Republic of Korea remains relatively peaceful. The Association has credited this to 'their efforts to maintain social stability.' Netizens have responded by questioning whether Ego-stream is actually the Association..."]

Of course, only Korea was peaceful—the rest of the world was on the verge of falling apart.

-Click.

"Sigh..."

Lee Seol-ah sighed after hanging up the phone.

Busy, busy. Even ten bodies wouldn't be enough.

During this unexpected global crisis, Lee Seol-ah was having a better time than anyone.

With repeated security collapses and terrorist attacks overseas, corporate values were gradually declining.

Of course, the Republic of Korea was the exception, remaining peaceful without any crisis. Using capital she had stockpiled in advance, she was shopping for foreign companies. She had bought so many through various tricks that she was almost running out of funds.

"I wonder if this is enough?"

Having roughly completed the situation, she leaned back in her chair. She let out a sigh, but still wore a satisfied smile. She wasn't sure if such an opportunity would come again, but she had done as much as she could. Although she couldn't acquire many Korean companies since they were less affected and defended their stock prices well... still, it was better than nothing.

"...Really, I don't know what I would have done without Da-in."

Lee Seol-ah muttered without realizing it.

Egostic. Thanks to his advance warning, she had been able to prepare and cope with everything, so she was grateful.

...Besides, thanks to him, only the Republic of Korea had avoided disaster, which was fortunate.

...But really, what would have happened without Egostic?

Just thinking about that possibility was terrifying.

First, Seoul would have been destroyed by Behemoth and the Han-eun Group, and then the Moonlight Church would have delivered the final blow for a clean finish. Seoul would have been completely ruined, and Busan might have become the capital.

"Wait, but that would be good...?"

If the Blue House and National Assembly had moved to Busan, she might have already taken control of the government. Since Yu-seong Corporation's headquarters was in Busan... perhaps the Republic of Korea would have long since fallen into her hands.

"...But that's not right."

However, she shook her head. Lee Seol-ah wanted to obtain a sound and intact Republic of Korea, not a collapsing country.

Probably, if she had easily taken over the Republic of Korea in a world without Egostic...

...Perhaps her counterpart in that world wouldn't have been very happy.

Rather, in a situation without goals, she might have been suffering.

Well, it was all just absurd fantasy.

Fortunately, Egostic existed, and because of that, the Republic of Korea was still functioning properly today.

'...Egostic.'

In fact, when Egostic first appeared, she hadn't been particularly interested in him. She just thought he was one of many common villains.

But as time passed and she began to observe his actions.

She changed her mind.

Now she sincerely thought he was amazing.

He knew all the information that even she didn't know, and the way he gathered talented people—powerful ability users—and built his own power from scratch was truly something she should learn from. On top of that, hadn't he already prevented several crises in the Republic of Korea?

She didn't understand why someone who seemed to be protecting the country more than anyone else would call himself a villain, but there must be a deeper meaning to it. Da-in seemed to know almost everything.

The problem was that all his attention was focused only on Stardust.

"....."

...Of course, as time continued to pass, she had thought that she might be able to make him notice her rather than Stardust, who didn't even know where he lived.

But then there were all those female villains hanging around him...

But none of that mattered—the biggest problem was that she had made a big mistake last time, and he was disappointed in her.

"...Right, let's just stay put for now."

...Still, she was the only hero who knew his identity, so there was still hope. Maybe...

As Lee Seol-ah was trying to comfort herself, her face suddenly hardened at a passing thought.

"...Come to think of it, Shadow Walker, that guy too..."

When the Moonlight Church's monster awoke, Stardust almost figured out that Egostic had defeated it.

While Lee Seol-ah was trying to cover it up, surprised inside.

At that time, Shadow Walker strongly testified that he hadn't seen anyone, successfully making Stardust think she was mistaken.

"...Did he really not see anything?"

Egostic must have been there. If he had looked around, there's no way he wouldn't have seen him.

...Then, why did he so strongly claim that he hadn't seen anything?

After getting up for a moment, taking a wine bottle from the cabinet, filling it with grape juice, and taking a sip to cool her head, she carefully speculated.

"Maybe..."

She hadn't thought about it at the time because she was busy, but now that she thought about it...

Could Egostic have approached him like he had approached her?

It was entirely possible, especially since Egostic had been the one to approach her first...

"...I should talk to Shadow."

She made up her mind.

Let's see, where was Kim Ja-hyun's phone number...
