Chapter 125 - Terrible Calamity

\# Dark Skies Over Seoul

It was an ordinary afternoon in the Republic of Korea.

Just like any other day, bright sunlight illuminated the city—an ordinary day.

Well.

To be precise, it had been an ordinary day.

At least until the moment after the leader of the Moonlight Cult delivered his message to the nation, when the sky suddenly darkened.

Dark skies.

Flying at maximum speed through the suddenly blackened heavens, Stardust shuddered with foreboding.

A supernatural phenomenon—the sky that had been bright just moments ago was now shrouded in darkness.

'...What on earth is happening?'

Currently, all communications in the Republic of Korea had been cut off, making it impossible to properly contact the Association.

Against the backdrop of the silent, pitch-black sky, she flew toward the mysterious, terrible sounds that kept echoing.

All she knew was that "something" had appeared in one part of Seoul that was now engulfed in darkness.

And after that something appeared, all contact from that part of the city had been lost.

Anyone could tell something serious was happening.

"......."

With that grave expression, Stardust, her blonde hair streaming behind her, flew through the pitch-black sky.

As she approached the scene,

she suddenly froze at the sight before her.

A city swallowed by pitch-black darkness.

Flying fast enough to see the city with her naked eyes, she could finally see "it" standing in the middle of downtown.

"...What is that thing?"

It was.

Truly the appearance of a calamity.

Witnessing this surreally horrific scene, Stardust clenched her fist again and charged at the massive purple entity.

For a moment when their eyes met, her head began to ring, but that was all. Unlike ordinary people, she wasn't lifted into the air screaming.

Gritting her teeth, she approached closer, and as the creature lunged at her again, she dodged it several times in mid-air while closing in. The creature also clenched its fist and began to engage Stardust.

KWANG.

And Stardust, facing off against the creature.

Gritted. Her teeth.

It was strong, too strong.

No matter how much she attacked, it seemed to take almost no damage, and the attacks it unleashed were almost lethal, perhaps due to the purple aura surrounding it.

This twisted, grotesque thing that resembled no living creature on Earth.

Even while battling her, it continued to lift nearby people with its supernatural power, extracting and absorbing something like their souls.

After exchanging blows with it several times, she intuitively realized.

...That she could never defeat this thing.

'...Why is this happening?'

Though she already sensed defeat.

Still, she charged at it.

...Even if she died here,

she would save even one more person before her final moment.

And so.

Stardust's life-or-death battle began.
