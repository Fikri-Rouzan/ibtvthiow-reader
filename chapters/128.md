...Did I really defeat it?

When she launched her attack, she definitely heard a bang- sound, like a gunshot.

And someone was clearly standing on the rooftop.

...And that someone was probably.

"Anyway, Stardust. I'll give you a bonus too, so get some good rest, okay? Go on a trip somewhere. I heard there's a warm sea on the east coast despite this weather, go somewhere like that and rest. You deserve a reward for your hard work. You're a national star now, hahaha!!!"

"Hmm.... Um, ah? Oh, yes. Stardust. Your fight was truly magnificent. Get some rest now. You're the hero of Korea, aren't you? Icicle said she'll take care of everything else."

"Excuse me, *hiccup*. Ja-hyun? When did I say that?"

As the Director, followed by Icicle and Shadow Walker, all addressed her this way, Stardust's feelings now bordered on guilt.

...Yes. It's true that she fought to the end, risking her life.

But was she really the one who finished it?

The bang- sound, the shadow on the rooftop.

Someone who knew more than anyone else, who used a gun as their main weapon, seemed to wear a mask, and had always saved her in moments of crisis.

At the same time, the image of a man flashed through her mind, but she shook her head.

...That's too much speculation. Maybe I just misheard and missaw things because I was exhausted at the end. There's no evidence other than her intuition. Besides, he's a villain.

However.

Shin Ha-ru's intuition strongly insisted on one thing.

...That it wasn't something she could have defeated.

Someone else must have helped.

Probably, surely it was him...

"Haha! To think you defeated that monster single-handedly, Stardust. You really are..."

"Director."

Interrupting the excited Director.

"...No matter how I think about it, I don't think I was the one who defeated it."

The moment her words ended.

The atmosphere suddenly froze.

"...What did you say?"

As the Director's face hardened at her bombshell statement.

There were people even more surprised than him.

"......*Hiccup*."

".....!"

They were Icicle and Shadow Walker, who were now wearing pale expressions.

'....Egostic. He definitely said he didn't want his identity revealed.'

He recalled the memory from the rooftop that day.

The man who adamantly refused to be known, who wanted to continue living in darkness as a villain.

And the man who kept coughing up blood, again and again.

From there, Shadow Walker formed a hypothesis.

'....Is he terminally ill?'

Why does that man choose to live as a villain rather than a hero, doing good deeds while willingly accepting the stigma of being a villain in the eyes of the world?

Why would someone who seemed to care about this world and its people more than anyone else do that?

...Could it be that he's terminally ill, and is preparing for people not to lose hope if he dies while active as a hero?

Since he has a predetermined day to leave... not getting attached. Quietly protecting the world from behind as a villain.

'....Sniff. Egostic. You truly are a real man.'

A dark hero protecting the world from the shadows... Egostic...

Having created an epic saga in his mind, Shadow Walker reached a conclusion.

Yes. For the sake of the true hero Egostic, I can at least do this much.

Don't worry, Egostic. Leave this to me.

Possessed by this conviction, Shadow Walker brazenly told Stardust again.

"I checked all around you just before you were about to defeat it. No one was near you. Of course, there was no one on any rooftop either."

"....That can't be."

"Trust me, Stardust. You know that even when my abilities are weakened in daylight, I can still notice that much."

"There was no one around you then. You're the one who defeated it."

"That's right, Ha-ru. Who else could have been there? Of course you defeated it. You must be seeing things because you're exhausted. Should I recommend a good resort?"

"......."

"...Hahaha! Yes, Stardust, you must be mistaken. I was really surprised for a moment. Well, I need to go explain to the reporters what kind of help our association provided, so I'll be off."

"...I'll be going too. Being awake during the day really makes me sleepy. ...By the way, Icicle, is that alcohol?"

"What? I need to drink this regularly these days or I'll go crazy, okay?"

"....You've been hiccupping all this time... That's... No, never mind. Do as you please."

As their voices gradually faded away.

Soon, only Stardust remained in the conference room.

"......"

In the place everyone had left.

Left alone, Stardust continued to mutter with a serious face.

".....But it wasn't me, it couldn't have been...."

A situation where everyone except her seemed to be in cahoots, telling her it was just a misunderstanding.

....A misunderstanding?

That's absurd, it doesn't make sense...

With everyone denying it, even she was becoming confused.

....Did I really just imagine it?

"....It really seemed like Egostic."

She admitted without realizing it.

...Yes, perhaps she had imagined it because she was too dependent on Egostic.

Wait. Dependent? Am I dependent on Egostic, a villain?

"......"

Whoosh.

Her face reddened without her realizing it, and she shook her head quickly to correct her thoughts.

That can't be. I'm just exhausted and imagined it.

...But no, it really seemed like Egostic. My intuition says so.

".....Am I delusional?"

No.

Still, it feels like Egostic was the one who defeated it.

She could only mutter words that no one would hear.

Yes.

....This time, I'll trust my intuition.

As she was thinking this, she suddenly came to her senses and looked around the empty conference room in bewilderment.

....Wait. Come to think of it, where did everyone go, leaving me alone?

And so Shin Ha-ru, sitting alone in the large empty office, dusted off her seat and left.

...They're all being too much.
