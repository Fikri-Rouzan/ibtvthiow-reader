\# Soul Devourer

A very intuitive nickname for this strange creature, it's one of the Moon Light Cult's ultimate weapons that appears in the final chapter of part 2 in the original work. In other words, it obviously first appears in the final chapter of part 2, so it shouldn't be showing up yet.

"....But why is it already here?"

I guess stealing Eun-wol, the Moon Priestess, in the middle of the terrorist attack through NTR was a pretty big deal.

Hmm. Did they just stage another attack because I interfered?

You just made me nervous for nothing.

So, game over.

"....What should I do now?"

No. Honestly, I could just fire one large silver bullet and end it all. In the original work, they were only troublesome before people knew silver was their weakness. Yeah. This is actually good.
