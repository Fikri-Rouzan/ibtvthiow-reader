Maybe it's because I'm returning to terrorism after more than half a year, but I'm feeling somewhat nostalgic. Standing at the top of the blue sky, surrounded by cameras and speaking like this—it's been a really long time.

"I actually wasn't planning to appear again after last time... but I received a great opportunity from Celeste to conquer the Republic of Korea together! Now, as an executive of Kathedal, I'll be facing all of you."

I glanced at the rapidly scrolling chat and tried to maintain my composure.

...Well, it's not our first meeting... we already met last time and did all sorts of things.

Of course, I couldn't let that slip, so I tried my best to act as if nothing had happened.

And in the meantime.

"Egostic..."

"Hello, Ms. Stardus. It's been a while!"

Stardus.

Finally, she arrived before me.

"..."

"....."

The moment our eyes met, she gave me an affectionate look that only I could see.

As if it never happened, she immediately hardened her expression again.

Then she spoke to me in an icy cold voice, just as frigidly as before.

"...You. Didn't you say you would never appear before me again? Why are you standing in front of me now?"

A voice so cold it could freeze just by hearing it.

...It was perfect acting—anyone who didn't know better would immediately be convinced she hated me.

To be honest, it gave me chills...

Of course, I just responded to her words with a playful smile.

"Hahaha! This is embarrassing. I actually intended to do that, but Kathedal offered me such a good opportunity. They said they would fully support me in conquering the Republic of Korea—how could I refuse?"

"You worthless trash..."

At my words, she muttered in a low voice with a stern face.

...But the problem was that from here on, her acting seemed to become difficult, as she started giving me apologetic looks.

I should probably end this conversation soon. Yeah. This should be enough of an alibi.

As if reading my thoughts.

Stardus soon began speaking to me in a cold voice.

"Whatever, that's enough. I will end your villainy here and now."

With those words.

Behind her, the silhouettes of various people began to appear one by one.

They were dozens of heroes who had followed Stardus to capture me.

More specifically...

They were members led by my Ego-squad members, whom I had notified in advance.

Now it's time to start the play.

Looking at the many heroes standing before me.

"Haha! It seems quite a lot of you have come just to catch me... Actually, Kathedal has provided me with quite a bit of support."

"So, let's see if you can stop this army of heaven."

In front of them.

I stood proudly with my arms crossed, my cape fluttering.

A situation where dozens of heroes faced hundreds of soldiers.

A tense atmosphere where a fight could break out at any moment.

And this situation.

'...Everything is going according to plan.'

It was all intentional.

Originally, I no longer needed to actively carry out terrorism. Now that Stardus's power was on track, I would have already retired under normal circumstances. I would have just given orders from behind the scenes, not appearing at the forefront like now.

However.

...As Stardus and I somehow developed this kind of relationship, the situation changed.

This was all just a show anyway.

I just needed to show the world that I was fulfilling my mission as a Kathedal executive in the invasion of Korea, and that the Association was successfully stopping me.

That's how this conspiracy began.

The conclusion I ultimately reached was this:

Have the Ego-squad, that is, the heroes, fight against my army of heaven.

...More precisely, I ordered both sides to just pretend to fight spectacularly, like a mock training exercise. There was no need to be serious. We just needed to deceive the eyes of the world.

And.

If Stardus and I disappeared under the pretext of fighting separately in this situation...?

Ta-da.

The birth of a legitimate hero-villain meeting.

I told this plan to Stardus, and she readily agreed. After all, it was a good story where no casualties would occur.

...And it was also an opportunity for us to meet.

So, according to plan, I started the grand show.

"Very well. You want to fight. Go, my soldiers of heaven. Show those arrogant heroes a lesson!"

I commanded my heavenly soldiers.

"This is war!"

The battle between heroes and Kathedal soldiers began.

...More precisely, since it was a battle between Ego-squad members and my summons, it was a battle where they pretended to fight fiercely without killing each other.

"I'll focus on capturing Egostic! All other heroes concentrate on dealing with those monsters!"

"You!"

And our figures were not seen again until the battle ended.
