Villain.

What is a villain?

Primarily found in creative works, a villain is an evil character who opposes the protagonist. In particular, main villains continue to oppose the protagonist until the end, posing a constant threat. As a bonus, they often appear as terrifying masterminds who make you wonder if the protagonist can actually win.

And when I think about it now, I could be considered a main villain. I mean, if I'm not the main villain after opposing Stardust until the end and even being acknowledged by her as her archenemy, then what am I?

So our group, the most influential villain alliance in the Republic of Korea, composed entirely of ability users...

"What is this?"

"It's cotton candy."

...We were making cotton candy.

Well, people need to enjoy sweet things once in a while.

In the garden in front of the mansion. The garden that our Death Knight completely renovated when he said he needed a hobby.

Choi Se-hee and Seo Ja-young had somehow acquired a cotton candy machine from somewhere.

And so, an unexpected cotton candy feast was unfolding in front of the garden.

"....This is quite fascinating."

The most interested was our centuries-old spirit.

Dressed in white traditional clothes with a hairpin, the sight of her eating cotton candy with sparkling eyes was quite incongruous.

Of course, I was also holding a cotton candy.

Pink cotton candy. When I tore off a piece and put it in my mouth, it melted softly.

...I think it's been a very long time since I've had this, probably since visiting an amusement park as a child.

"Hahaha! It's time for free cotton candy distribution! Everyone line up!"

As I listened to Death Knight enthusiastically making cotton candy behind the machine after learning how to use it,

I asked Seo Ja-young, who was tearing into a cotton candy the size of a house beside me.

"Where on earth did you find this?"

"Hmm? I saw it for sale online. So I just bought one."

Seo Ja-young replied while lying on the bench and putting another piece of cotton candy in her mouth.

With her eyes closed and a happy smile as she munched away, she seemed to really enjoy the cotton candy.

"Oppa. What are you doing there? Come over here."

Just then, someone grabbed my arm and pulled me.

I looked to see Han Seo-eun holding a sky-blue cotton candy.

"Try this flavor too. It's delicious."

She naturally tore off a piece of her cotton candy and put it in my mouth.

The cotton candy naturally melted and dispersed in my mouth.

"How is it?"

"It's good. Sweet."

To be honest, I couldn't taste any difference between the pink and blue ones. I was told one was strawberry flavor and this was blueberry... I wonder why?

Anyway, they were both sweet and delicious.

And so, this impromptu picnic unfolded in front of the mansion.

It started with a cotton candy machine, but everyone was outside with smiles blooming on their faces. It was nice to see.

While we were fully relaxing with blankets spread out,

Seo-eun, who had been busy taking pictures of people eating cotton candy, suddenly asked me.

"Oppa, I was thinking..."

"Yes?"

"What do you think about us trying social media?"

"Social media?"

When I asked what she meant, Seo-eun began to explain step by step.

To summarize, she suggested creating an official Ego-stream account to increase our visibility and post pictures of our terrorist activities or daily life. For example, posting pictures of moments like this with our faces hidden.

"...And if she sees us like this, hee. That woman..."

I couldn't hear the last part because it was too quiet, but I imagine it was something similar.

Anyway, as Seo-eun spoke with sparkling eyes, I fell into thought.

...Well. I wonder if there's any need for it. My recognition in Korea is already extremely high, and globally, my existence isn't really known outside of Japan. Plus, managing that would just take up time.

"I don't think it's necessary right now?"

"Hmm... okay."

Seo-eun seemed slightly disappointed, but I reassured her by saying I might reconsider if the situation changes.

...And, thinking about it more, it might not be a bad idea to run one after I retire from terrorism.

Anyway, as we continued chatting about various things, I was relaxing while drinking tea that Su-bin had brought.

....I wondered if it was okay to be eating cotton candy and relaxing like this in these chaotic times, but well. Sometimes you need to rest to work harder later. We'll have to go on another expedition to catch villains soon anyway.

As I was resting,

"Hmm...?"

I received a call from Lee Seol-ah.
