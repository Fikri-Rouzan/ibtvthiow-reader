\# Celestial Moon Emperor

Celestial Moon Emperor.

As the founder of the Moon Light Cult that worships the Moon God, he aims to connect Earth with a new dimension, summoning gods and monsters from another world to cleanse the current Earth and create a new world.

In the original story, he used the Moon Priestess to devastate Seoul to raise his profile, and after extensive research, he finally opened a dimensional gate to summon monsters from another dimension.

The starting point was the Moon Priestess's first terrorist attack.

After being manipulated by the Celestial Moon Emperor to carry out numerous attacks, she ultimately dies at Stardust's hands.

And in this scene, the Moon Priestess's secret is revealed.

She too was merely a victim exploited by the Celestial Moon Emperor.

"*Cough* Please... just kill me..."

Stardust, who could never even approach the magical storms she created, finally destroys all the debuff magic circles and reaches the eye of the storm.

And at the center was the Moon Priestess, controlled by the Celestial Moon Emperor.

Despite not wanting to harm people, she had been forced to commit acts of terrorism by the Celestial Moon Emperor. In the end, she begs Stardust to kill her, saying that only her death would end everything.

As a result, Stardust finally kills her, and the terrorist attack that half-destroyed Seoul comes to an end.

And when the Celestial Moon Emperor hears that the Moon Priestess has died, his response is:

"Is that so? Then I guess I won't be able to conquer areas beyond Seoul."

This was possible because he never treated the Moon Priestess as a person, but merely as a living weapon.

Anyway, this episode was one of the most criticized by fans.

Heroes were helplessly slaughtered while doing nothing, the Moon Priestess kept appearing not just once but five times endlessly, and even the ending was unsatisfying. Seoul was already destroyed, the Moon Priestess died pitifully, and the Moon Light Cult continued to exist unscathed. On top of that, the protagonist Stardust remained powerless throughout the episode and fell into depression at the end, making fans cough up blood from frustration.

Although it ended with a hopeful scene of Seoul being rebuilt, that was it.

It was an episode that brought tears throughout the reading.

At least the previous VK Corporation's giant weapon episode ended after a few issues, but this episode dragged on for over ten issues. For months, fans just watched Stardust getting beaten up. As a Stardust fan, it was a time of endless tears.

But now.

Things are different.

"Oppa, what are we going to do with these magic circles?"

"Let's leave them for now."

In the original story, the Association had to beg America to bring in American Exorcists to remove them.

But I have domestic cooperation.

Vine Witch. I'm counting on you...!!

After discovering the locations of the magic circles, which are crucial for dealing with the Moon Priestess, I felt relieved.

If I take care of this, I can avoid the depressing trigger for a while, right? Right?

And for that, the most important thing is to take the Moon Priestess myself if possible.

"..."

As someone who shed buckets of tears when she asked Stardust to kill her in the original story, this is only natural. She needs to find some happiness...!!

At least I have the divine healer Lee Ha-yul, so we can somewhat resolve the curse that the Celestial Moon Emperor placed on her.
